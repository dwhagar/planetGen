0. How the numbers were obtained

  • Five reading workers each read their papers (text layer where it exists; page images for the scans, which are King, Bates, Tabazadeh, Zeleznik and Myhre). They marked V = read from the paper's own text or table image, R = recomputed by them (arithmetic in their file), D = digitized from the geometry of a figure, FIG = read off a plotted curve (about plus or minus 10 to 20 percent). I use the same marks below. Nothing is filled from memory except where a file says RECALLED.
  • I re-read these myself against the PDFs and they agree with the workers: Hassler (0.210 mGy/day, 0.64 mSv/day, Q 3.05, 76 mGy/yr, 232 mSv/yr); Palme and O'Neill Table 3 oxides and total 98.41, Th 83.4 ppb, U 21.8 ppb; Aumer and Binney beta values and the Table 2 best-fit row; Bierson and Nimmo Table 1 (920, 3500, 0.60, 0.20, -0.1, -0.11); Sneep and Ubachs Table 2; Irwin 0.4-0.8 ppm and 4.4-5.0 times solar; Graus Eq. 1 with V50 = 18 km/s and sigma 2.5; Tabazadeh Eq. 1 and Table 1 rows 10 to 40 (page image); Mumma Table 4, SO2 row 0.2; the first page of Myhre. Numbers I did not re-read stay with the worker's V mark and are not independently double-checked.

2. The numbers the gap plan needs

2.1 AD4 Rayleigh (GEN.422)

King factor: F_K = (6 + 3 rho)/(6 - 7 rho), rho = natural-light depolarization ratio ([R2361: Eq. 78 and 90], p. 351 and 353; Sneep Eq. 7, p. 296; Bucholtz Eq. 6). [V] The mixture rule from King Eq. 91 and 94: F_mix - 1 = sum over gases of w_r (F_r - 1), with w_r = v_r (mu_r - 1)^2 / sum(v (mu - 1)^2), equivalently sigma_mix = sum v_r sigma_r. [R, worker A] Inverse: rho = 6 (F - 1)/(3 + 7 F). [R]

2.10 BC7 ice compaction and density (GEN.442, 457)

Bierson and Nimmo (accepted manuscript), Table 1 and Eq. 1-2: ice density 920 and silicate density 3500 kg/m^3, initial porosity 0.60, strength-supported porosity 0.20 (ice), compaction parameters b1 = -0.1 per MPa (ice) and b2 = -0.11 (silicate). Ice porosity phi_i = max(0.60 exp(-0.1 P), 0.20), P in MPa; silicate phi_s = min(0.6 P^-0.11, 0.6). A constant 70 percent rock mass fraction matches 14 of 17 Kuiper Belt objects within 2 sigma; minimum formation time 4 Myr (rock fraction 0.5) or 5 Myr (0.7), from early Al-26. Predictions in one sentence each (p. 10): Makemake and 2007 OR10 similar to or slightly below Pluto (about 1800 kg/m^3), MU69 about 750. [V; the first three Table 1 values and the porosity parameters re-read by me] Contradiction: our ice compaction guess has a reference pressure P0 = 1e6 Pa, ten times lower than the paper's exp(-0.1 P) (P0 = 10 MPa) with a 0.2 floor; the paper wins. Our 0.08 scatter and 0.58-0.82 range are not in the paper. Eq. 3 (dphi/dt = phi P/eta) is printed with a positive sign, which would make porosity grow, and the file carries a stray note "error in equation 2b": check against [R0372] or the published version before coding Eq. 3. Fray and Schmitt (ice densities, vapour-pressure windows) is paid and not here.

Star formation fitted with the best total fit: alpha -2.549, gamma 0.117 per Gyr, tau_max 12.602 Gyr. beta = 0.5 is excluded and "the very upper limit" (Table 3). GR6 and GR3 thick disc, Milky Way. [R1863]: thick disc forms from about 13 to about 8 Gyr ago (4 to 5 Gyr long), the age scale uncertain by 1 to 1.5 Gyr; inner thin disc starts about 8 Gyr ago, outer thin disc 9 to 10 Gyr ago (so "thin-disc start" is two numbers); M_thick/M_thin at least 0.2, possibly up to about 1. [V] Aumer and Binney's disc age is 12.6 Gyr (tau_max). [R1649] gives disc density forms from which thick/thin ratios of 0.24 and 0.29 follow [R]; it has no star formation or metallicity formulas, no bulge, no halo. [R3531] covers one galaxy (FCC 170): star formation peaks at the 14 Gyr edge of its age grid (resolution about 3 Gyr), thick-disc history has a second bump near 10 Gyr and stops about 11 Gyr, and no thick/thin ratio is printed (the worker's digitized 0.45 is a bin-mass ratio, not a disc ratio; its galaxy mass is internally inconsistent). The plan's statements about FCC 153, FCC 177 and a "2 to 4 Gyr first phase" need [R3532] (not uploaded) and [R0349] (not uploaded) and stay unverified.

GR15 occupation width (R15). Graus et al. v1 preprint, Eq. 1: the occupation is an erf in peak velocity, not in mass: f(>Vpeak) = (1 + erf((Vpeak - V50)/(sqrt2 sigma)))/2 with V50 = 18 km/s and sigma = 2.5 km/s (alternative V50 = 10.5 km/s), sigma assumed from FIRE-2, not fitted. [V; re-read by me] Converting to mass with Mpeak proportional to Vpeak^3 anchored at 1e9 solar masses at 20 km/s gives widths of about 0.18 dex (V50 = 18) and 0.32 dex (V50 = 10.5) [R]. The conventional model's edge-to-edge span (25 to 10 km/s) is 1.19 dex against the 1.22 dex between the Benitez-Llambay and Frenk edges, so the plan's derived width is confirmed, not tightened. No reionization redshift is used, so GR12 is not served. The v1 numbers may differ from the published MNRAS 488, 4585.

Knapen et al. book. All 10 chapters present. Qualitative or second-hand for GR1, GR3, GR6, GR10, GX5; nothing for GR5, GR7 to GR9, GR11 to GR15 or the GX5 baseline. Two conflicts with our files: its [R0864] statement (hot-to-cold mass ratio independent of galaxy mass) disagrees with the mass-dependent [R0860] law the plan uses, and its [R3041] halo-fraction scatter (1.01 dex, 8 galaxies) is about three times the Harmsen 0.33 dex in the plan. 2.12 Ice-giant composition (not a listed gap)

Irwin et al. (author manuscript): H2S above the main Uranus cloud (1.2 to 3 bar) is 0.4 to 0.8 ppm at the cloud top, column (2 to 5) x 10^19 molecules per cm^2, polar cap not detected; deep S/N above 4.4 to 5.0 times solar; the cloud is probably H2S ice, not NH3. Deep CH4 4 percent and He:H2 = 0.131 are assumed inputs, not retrievals. Relative humidity depends on the temperature profile (113 percent with one profile, 16 percent with the Spitzer profile), so use the cloud-top mole fraction. Nothing in the plan carries a number to compare. [V; re-read by me]
