What the check shows, in four lines:

  • Numbers copied from a real table or figure (the Illustris and D'Souza curves at low mass, the thick and thin disk ranges, the globular cluster size 2.7 pc, the nuclear cluster mass range) are close or right.
  • Numbers written with a plus-or-minus that look fitted (the age-velocity normalisations, the occupation center 8.6, the nuclear cluster exponent 0.65, the halo formation redshift line, the stellar-to-halo zero point) are mostly in no source, and where a source was read they are wrong by 0.3 to 0.9 dex or more.
  • Four attributions are wrong in a way that matters: the 10^8.6 occupation center is Benitez-Llambay and Frenk's no-reionization limit, the 1.9 stellar-to-halo slope is [R1522, R1523]'s modification of [R0320], the nuclear dispersion exponent 0.25 is the Neumayer occupation fraction law, and the [R3531, R3532] thick disk ages cover three Fornax S0 galaxies.
  • Three places where two read sources disagree with each other need Boss (section 5).

2. How the check was done

Papers were downloaded from arXiv, pdftotext was used for the text, and curves in figures were read from the vector paths of the PDF (pdftocairo -svg), calibrated on the axis tick marks and validated against numbers printed in each paper's own text. Labels: V read in the text of the paper, D computed or digitised here from a V source (script scripts/compute_checks.py, output compute_checks.out.txt), 2nd read only through another paper's account of it, GEN.406 taken from that research note where it had read the paper. Where two sources were read the one with the better treatment is named.

Papers opened (arXiv ids): [R3790] (1511.08804), [R1169] (1404.2123), [R0338] (2004.06124), [R3273] (2001.03626), [R3209] (1806.06891), [R0865] (1709.00457), [R0860] (2101.04478), [R0861] and 2012 (1108.0037, 1209.1513), [R3531] and b (1901.04310, 1904.01260), [R3530] proceedings (2301.10666), [R3928] (2410.05761), [R0908] (astro-ph/0610721), [R1811] (1409.6306), [R2268] (1602.01838), [R0090] (0901.3553), [R0090] (0812.3405), [R2287] (1905.11414), [R1027] (1601.07905), [R0894] (1409.5228), [R1523] (1404.5313), ELVIS (1310.6746), [R0320] (1207.6105), [R3730] (2101.12216), [R2917] (1307.8130), [R2193] (astro-ph/0508219). [R0876] (0910.3211) and [R0097] (1701.02741) were fetched and not examined. The [R0865] catalogue (VizieR J/A+A/610/A5, table B, 124 galaxies) was parsed by the VizieR Courteau and Comerón thread (/mnt/project-files/reports/vizier-courteau-comeron-2026-10-17-files/). 5. Conflicts between two read sources (Boss to decide)

Standing rule: the verified source wins over a contradicting one. Here both are verified, so the choice belongs to Boss.

  1. Thick/thin scale height ratio. [R4824]: 2.5 ± 30 percent. [R0865] catalogue: median 4.24 (mean 4.61, 124 galaxies, VizieR). The two fits are different surveys and decomposition methods, and Comerón's has the better point-spread-function treatment. Gemini's range sits on the Yoachim side.
  2. Thick/thin mass ratio against Vc. Yoachim: 0.53 (Vc/100)^-2.3. Comerón catalogue: 0.87 (Vc/100)^-1.09 with 0.27 dex scatter. They differ by a factor of 1.6 at 100 km/s and a factor of 5 at 255 km/s. The VizieR thread's output also shows 48 of the 124 catalogue galaxies above the plan's hard bound; that is its finding and has not been re-run here.
  3. Cluster system radius against galaxy radius. [R1408, R2038]: about 3.5 and 3.7 ± 0.4. [R2268] Eq 5: about 6 (12 galaxies, slope 6.5 ± 1.3). Definitions and samples differ.
