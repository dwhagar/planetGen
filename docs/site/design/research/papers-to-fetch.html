How each field was obtained:

  • Citation and DOI: Crossref API (api.crossref.org/works?query.bibliographic=..., or a direct /works/<doi> lookup to confirm a DOI). A DOI is listed only when Crossref returned a record whose title and year match the paper. Otherwise the cell says "DOI not confirmed". The Crossref calls went through the WebFetch tool (Bash curl to Crossref was denied by the permission classifier), so each response was transcribed by a small model asked to quote verbatim. DOIs, titles, years and pages agreed across repeated queries, but a spot check of a few DOIs by Boss is cheap.
  • Free copy and preprint links: OpenAlex API (api.openalex.org, keyed by the confirmed DOI) and arXiv API searches. "bronze" means the publisher serves a free-to-read PDF without a licence. Links are OpenAlex's, not opened by me unless stated.
  • "Gap ids" are the ids of the gap-filling plan (BC, AD, GR) and the GEN items it names.
