Status: built (GEN.554 slice 1). The mass islands are explosion-islands.md (slice 2) and the kick step's ejected share natal-kick.md (slice 3); the neutron star mass function is a later slice. Code: galaxy/remnant_density.py (the black hole density of black-hole-density.md, now taking the remnant kind), galaxy/structure.py and galaxy/structure_draw.py (the galaxy's neutron star unbound share), galaxy/ejecta_field.py, galaxy/profile.py, and the scatter, reach and fill sites that read the kind factors.

What changed

Before: every galaxy rolled neutron stars at a fixed 4.0e-3 per star (tuning.PHENOMENON_RATE_PER_STAR, from Boss's research density of 1e9 in 2.5e11, GEN.100), whatever its metals. A black hole needs a massive star that fails to explode (black-hole-density.md), and that star would otherwise have made a neutron star, so the two counts were not independent and the old fixed rate could not follow the black hole draw.

After: every star of 8 to 150 solar masses ends as a neutron star or a black hole (the IMF share, 0.713% of the stars born since GEN.927; 0.637% on the Kroupa function). A place's neutron stars per star are the black hole sum with 1 - F_p in place of F_p:

r_NS(place) = IMF_SHARE x (1 - u) x sum over populations p [ w_p(place) x C_p x (1 - F_p(place)) ]

F_p is the failed-supernova share of black-hole-density.md (the same two drawn anchors per galaxy), C_p the same completeness share, and u the galaxy's neutron star unbound share (slice 1 drew it uniform over 0.2 to 0.4; it is now derived from the kick law and the galaxy's escape speed, natal-kick.md, and is a field of the galaxy's structure so the one value feeds both the bound count and the ejected count). The ejected count is formed x u, so bound plus ejected is what the galaxy formed. A galaxy that fails more massive stars (a higher black hole share) makes fewer neutron stars, and the black hole cap of the GEN.563 card ("Cap f so the sum fits") now holds by construction: neutron stars plus black holes formed never exceed the massive stars.

Numbers

Middle draw, Milky Way-like shape: a standalone sector rolls 4.66e-3 neutron stars per star against Boss's research density of 4.0e-3 (16% over, because the natal kick ejects 16% of the neutron stars where the old 20% to 40% range centered on 30%). The old 4.0e-3 is a test target (within 20%), no longer an input. Black holes at the standalone rate are unchanged (5.8e-4 per star). Only the neutron star counts move, and only through the galaxy's drawn shares, so generation-output changes.

Stand-ins and open items

  • The shares are the black hole draws (black-hole-density.md stand-ins: uniform across the verified range, linear in Z). No new tunable.
  • Magnetars still use MAGNETAR_PER_NEUTRON_STAR times the tuning rate (a stand-in); they follow the young stars, not the metals.
  • Later slice: the neutron star mass function. The progenitor windows are explosion-islands.md; the natal kick's ejected share is natal-kick.md (the placement of the kicked stars is still remnant_distribution.py's empirical factors). The cap stays under the recommended option until Boss's card answer says otherwise.

Tests

tests/test_neutron_star_density.py: neutron stars and black holes add up to the formed remnants per population, bound plus ejected is what the galaxy formed, a higher black hole share leaves fewer neutron stars, a metal-rich place makes more, the solar-circle rate is within 25% of 4.0e-3, the profile's factor is the place's and within the bound, and only the stellar black holes and plain neutron stars follow the metals.
