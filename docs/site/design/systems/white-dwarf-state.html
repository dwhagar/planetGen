A white dwarf as a star: how hot, how bright, how large, how crystallised (GEN.742)

docs/design/systems/white-dwarf-state.md

Status (2026-10-24): built (datasets/white_dwarf_models.py cooling_state, physics/stellar_evolution.py _white_dwarf_state, tests/test_white_dwarf_cooling.py, tests/test_white_dwarf_cooling_real.py). Row S3 of reports/physics-gaps-2026-10-24.md.

A white dwarf has no fusion and only cools. Its luminosity used to be a power law of the cooling age (L = 1e-3 (M / 0.6) t^-1.4, clamped to 1e-5 to 50 solar luminosities, the Mestel law) and its radius one zero-temperature mass-radius relation, so a star of a given mass had one radius whatever its age, a hydrogen layer made no difference, and the core never crystallised. Now all four come from the cooling sequences the setup step already downloads for the star's makeup [R0308].

When the sequences are missing

The dataset states its own fallback (white_dwarf_models.DATASET.fallback). A run that disregards missing data (GEN.599) takes the old Mestel law and the Chandrasekhar-type radius, and fetch.stand_ins_used() lists "the Mestel power law of the white-dwarf cooling"; any other run refuses (GEN.590). The law is the labelled fallback (tuning.WD_COOLING_*), not a second model beside the sequences.

What it changes

Stored values: the temperature, luminosity and radius of every white dwarf the population model makes, so the generator epoch rises. A young white dwarf is hotter than before (up to about 110,000 K) and a 10 Gyr one cooler (about 3,000 to 4,000 K for 0.6 solar masses, below 2,000 K with a thin hydrogen layer); the radius no longer depends on the mass alone. A white dwarf now draws one more uniform (the atmosphere).

Not yet

  • The hydrogen-to-helium atmosphere change with temperature (BC19: DA fraction against effective temperature from the large catalogues) is still a fixed share.
  • Helium-core and oxygen-neon white dwarfs have layers (physics/white_dwarf_layers.py, GEN.743: an oxygen-neon core of 55 percent oxygen and 30 percent neon with the helium and hydrogen layers of [R0627]; a helium core with a thick hydrogen envelope; stand-ins WL1 to WL6 in 6.197), but still no sequences of their own: the radius of both is the nearest carbon-oxygen sequence scaled by the zero-temperature relation (WL7).
  • A white dwarf placed by a user-given type (-star_type D...) and the companions of multistar.py still use the old mass-radius relation; the population model is the generator's path for a white dwarf primary.
  • Star makeup (stellar_composition.star_makeup) draws its own atmosphere from the star's key; the star's drawn atmosphere should replace it (GEN.553).
