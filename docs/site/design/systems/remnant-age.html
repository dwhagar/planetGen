A neutron star's or stellar black hole's age (GEN.553)

docs/design/systems/remnant-age.md

Status (2026-10-21): built (physics/remnant_progenitors.collapse_age_gy, tests/test_remnant_age.py).

A neutron star or stellar-mass black hole used to draw its age uniform over 10 Gy (tuning.COMPACT_REMNANT_AGE_RANGE_GY), whatever stars lived around it. It is now a remnant of its population: its age is the time since the star that made it collapsed.

Step Rule
Progenitor the IMF (imf.sample) over the kind's windows of initial mass (explosion-islands.md), no lighter than the lightest star that can have collapsed inside the population's window (youngest_progenitor_sol)
Time to collapse the end of the progenitor's last giant phase, main_sequence_lifetime_gy * remnant_age_fraction (remnant_formation_time_gy, 10 to 40 Myr for a neutron star's progenitor)
Birth age as any star's: the population's window (population_age_range_gy, age_window_gy) and its age law (galaxy.age_law, GEN.546), no younger than the time to collapse
Remnant age birth age less time to collapse, never under COMPACT_REMNANT_AGE_RANGE_GY[0] (10 kyr)

The population is the one the sector fill gives the body from its place's mix (FillContext.apply_population), as it gives a free-floating brown dwarf. A remnant made without a place has the whole disk's window, 0 to 10 Gy, so a standalone neutron star is almost what it was. A bulge or halo remnant is 8 to 13 Gy old, which the uniform draw never gave.

What changes downstream

The pulsar chance falls with age (exp(-age / PULSAR_ACTIVE_LIFETIME_YR)), so a young population's neutron stars pulse and an old population's do not, and a magnetar is born only inside a magnetar's active life.

What it does not do

  • The progenitor is not stored. Only the age comes out of the draw; GEN.554 (derived counts) and the remnant row of the body draw keep the progenitor with the kind.
  • Intermediate-mass and supermassive black holes keep the uniform age: no star collapsed to them (their own origin rows).
  • A remnant given an age (the core of a supernova remnant, a scatter row's magnetar) keeps it.
  • The progenitor range overlap. A star of 20 to 30 solar masses is in both ranges; the kind is the scatter's, the progenitor only gives the age.
  • A remnant of a multi-star tree (multistar._remnant) already draws its progenitor inside the system's age; the same youngest_progenitor_sol serves both.
