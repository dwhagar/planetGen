1. What Boss decided

  • Atmosphere composition and the composition of planets, moons, stars and everything is the joint headline of 9.0, together with the atmosphere colour.
  • Two research lanes first, one for atmospheres and one for bodies (planets, moons, stars, remnants and the rest): the compounds, minerals and elements that make them up, what is there and all the statistics needed to use it in calculations. All notes go into the design documents.
  • Every body gets figures on its composition from actual science, and not made up where that can be avoided. Drawing the density and the other figures that are drawn today and then filling in a composition that fits is acceptable, and so is building from the most basic composition up; either works, and the design picks the simplest consistent one per kind of body.
  • 9.0 completes it, together with the atmosphere colour, and adds what becomes derivable: the atmospheric radiation (the atmosphere's transmission and greenhouse effect, since we know what is in the air) and the surface radiation (from the atmosphere and the ground composition).
  • The building blocks go back from 9.0 to 8.1 (as early as their blockers allow), then a collision and consolidation sweep merges items that do almost the same thing and suit one worker, then the issue clusters and all documents are updated.
  • Added 2026-10-17 at 1:40 AM EDT: every composition category is a gated table of possibilities with weighted probabilities that add up to 100 percent (section 4b).
  • The standing rule stays: nothing fixed per class and nothing copied from the Solar System or the Milky Way; every input is a stored physical quantity, a derived value or a seeded draw, and the Sun, Earth and the rest are test targets.

2. The chain

One chain from the element to the body to the derived value:

  1. The element table (GEN.438): atomic data and the radioactive isotopes. No abundances.
  2. The compound and material tables: the gases and compounds of atmospheres (GEN.410) and the minerals, ices, metals and envelopes of bodies (GEN.427), each with its stats and sources; molar masses come from the elements.
  3. The stellar composition relations (GEN.439) and their derived function (GEN.440): a star's composition from the stored [Fe/H], age and population, with no new column.
  4. The bodies: bulk fractions that drive density, mass and gravity (GEN.428, GEN.429), the surface composition (GEN.441), the small bodies (GEN.442), and the atmosphere's mixture (GEN.411) with ozone and aerosols.
  5. The derived values: the atmosphere colour (GEN.416 to GEN.419), the atmospheric radiation (GEN.443), the surface radiation (GEN.444), and what reads them (habitability, GEN.445; the text, GEN.446).

4b. The gated possibility table (Boss, 2026-10-17, 1:40 AM EDT)

Boss (typed in the project chat): for each composition there is a list of possibilities with weighted probabilities, for all compositions, each category a table of possibilities and their associated values; the list items are gated, each table line lists the planet or object classes it supports, and the composition is drawn from the probabilities, which add up to 100 percent. The coordinator answered yes with two refinements, kept here: a weight may depend on the star and the formation distance (the route 2 decision of section 4a), and every weight carries its source (nothing invented; the standing no-fixed-content rule).

The standing format (one loader, GEN.472; the sum test is TEST.214):

Column Meaning
possibility The row: a recipe, a taxon, an origin type, a population or a gas, named for what it is and never for a Solar System object
supported classes The class gate: the planet, moon, star or object classes the row applies to
weight or weight function A number, or a function of the star's stored [Fe/H], mass and age, the formation distance and the snow line
source Where the weight comes from (the F5 rule: the loader refuses a row without it). The licence is not a column: it is recorded in DATA-LICENSE.md in the repository root (Boss, 2026-10-22)
notes The ranges the row's drawn fractions or partial pressures take, the validity range, the check

The draw:

  1. Take the rows whose gate passes the body's class.
  2. Evaluate each weight at the body's star and formation distance; drop rows whose weight is zero.
  3. Normalise the rest to add up to 100 percent and pick one with the one random wrapper (GEN.56), in the table's fixed row order.
  4. The drawn row supplies the ranges of its fractions or partial pressures; those are normalised by the item that owns them (the mixture in GEN.411, the bulk fractions in GEN.428). Two sums are therefore checked: the weights of the possibilities, and the fractions of the drawn one.
  5. Proportions inside a drawn possibility (Decided 1:43 AM EDT, section 4a item 6): the table weights are used directly where the row names a sourced kernel, and as a Dirichlet draw around the weights where only a range is known, with the concentration from the spread, built from the one random wrapper.

Rules that follow:

  • No row passes the gate, or every weight is zero: an error that names the class and the table. There is no silent fallback to a fixed row.
  • A weight with no source is written open; the open rows of one gate share equally, the loader lists them in one report, and each is replaced when a source or a derivation is found. This is the one place the table holds a placeholder, and it is visible.
  • Adding, removing or reweighting a row changes the draws of every seed in its classes, so each table has a version and a change is a generator epoch bump.
  • A row built from a measured Solar System shape carries the marker stand-in (Decided 1:43 AM EDT, section 4a item 7), names its replacement research item (GEN.473 asteroid belt composition, GEN.474 rock types from the bulk oxides, GEN.475 meteorite groups as outputs, GEN.476 Al-26 and Fe-60 from the formation environment), and is listed with the open weights in the loader's report; the calibration rows themselves (Earth, Mars, Moon, the Galilean moons, Sirius B) live in test files only and no generator table reads them (TEST.215).
  • The materials and components are one shared table (Decided 1:47 AM EDT, section 4a item 8): a belt's, a comet's, a moon's and a planet's components are rows of the table of GEN.427 selected by the class gate, and the loader refuses a component list that belongs to one kind alone; the belt and field draw of today, with its own list of 84 names and per-family lists, is replaced by it (GEN.477).
  • One mixture storage and one text writer serve every body (the body composition research lane's section 14.6, proposed for the design GEN.426 to settle): a body's composition is a mixture of rows of the shared table stored as component mass fractions (DB.69 and DB.70 together, replacing asteroid_belt_composition and the planet composition text), the gate does the physics (condensation temperature at the formation distance, oxidation state, water and carbon inventory, star abundances) and not the object type, the rare-earth, uranium, platinum-group, gem and aromatic species are trace accessories scaled to the chemistry, and the descriptions come from the stored fractions through one thresholded writer (GEN.430, GEN.446), the class label becoming a summary of the mixture.
  • The source column carries the verification state (Decided 3:02 AM EDT, section 4a item 10): verified, partly verified with the limit the check named (the row ships as sourced-with-limit), open (no source), or stand-in; a value the checks found contradicted or unsupported is not a row's source, and when a partly verified and a verified source disagree the verified one is used. The loader reports the count of each state per table.
  • Tables register themselves with the loader, so TEST.214 walks them all and a table added later is covered without a change to the test.

Where it applies: the gas table (GEN.410, gate and source columns), the material table (GEN.427), the star abundance rows (GEN.439, GEN.440), the bulk recipes (GEN.428), the atmosphere recipes (GEN.411, GEN.454 to GEN.456), the small-body and moon tables (GEN.442, GEN.457, GEN.466) and the surface rows (GEN.441). Where an item is derived and not drawn (GEN.412, GEN.458 to GEN.462, GEN.464) it has no table and takes the drawn row or the weight arguments of the tables above. The element table (GEN.438) is a constants table with no weights.

As built (GEN.472)

  • Where. planetgen/generation/possibility_table.py: Row, Table, Registry (the module's REGISTRY is the generator's), Subject, Condition. The Dirichlet draw is draw.dirichlet (and draw.gamma) in util/draw.py, so it is built on the one random wrapper.
  • The gate evaluator. A row passes when the subject's class is in its classes (or the row says ANY_CLASS) and every Condition holds. A condition reads one stored quantity of the subject (minimum, maximum or equals, a bound inclusive) and a quantity the subject lacks is an error, never a pass. The condition predicates of section 14.6 (the condensation temperature at the formation distance, the oxidation state, the water and carbon inventory, the star's abundances) are conditions on those stored or derived quantities, so no table needs class letters.
  • Open weights. The sourced weights of the rows that pass are probabilities and the open rows of that gate share what is left over equally; with only open rows they share all of it. If the sourced weights already use up the whole, an open row gets nothing and stays in the report; if they add up to more than 1 while an open row passes, the draw is refused.
  • Fractions. A row with fractions and no concentration gives them directly (scaled to 1); with a concentration they are a Dirichlet draw around them (concentration_from_spread(mean, sd) gives the concentration for a known spread). Component names are rows of the one shared table (SHARED_COMPONENTS, "materials", GEN.427): a table with fractions must name it, and Registry.validate refuses a component that table does not hold, so no kind has a list of its own.
  • Versions. Table.digest hashes the version, every row's name, gate, weight (a function by its qualified name), state and fractions; Registry.versions lists (version, digest) per table. The first real tables (GEN.410, GEN.427, GEN.439) pin their table version; the 8.1 tables bump no epoch (Boss, 2026-10-17, 2:10 PM EDT, section 7.5), and the loader itself changes no stored value and has no epoch.
  • Not in the loader. The sum test over every class and table (TEST.214), the target-table check of the tunable rows (GEN.503) and the tables themselves.

7.1 The chain, as checked

  1. Elements (GEN.438): atomic data and the radioactive isotopes. A constants table, no weights, no abundances. As built (GEN.438): physics/elements.py loads three data files in physics/data/ (elements.json, isotopes.json, decay_series.json), built by scripts/build_element_tables.py from CIAAW's Atomic Weights 2021 (weights of the 118 elements, the abridged conventional value for the interval elements), NIST's isotope masses and natural compositions, NUBASE2020 (half-lives, decay modes and branchings, and the AME2020 mass excesses the chain steps' Q values come from), [R2768: Table 4] (the energy released per decay of 23 radionuclides, the whole chain for U-238, U-235 and Th-232) and [R1203: Table 2] (the four long-lived parents' heat constants, kept as the cross-check). Each file states its sources and the day it was read, and the loader refuses a file that does not (the licence is not a column: it is recorded in DATA-LICENSE.md). molar_mass(formula) is the one place a compound's molar mass is computed (kg/mol); heat_per_kg_of_isotope, specific_activity and isotopic_mass_fraction give the constants the radiogenic heat (GEN.459) and the ground's dose read. Where the sources differ the newer verified one is used: NUBASE2020's half-lives (U-238 4.463 Gyr against Dye's 4.468) and Lugaro's energies (K-40 1.05e-6 erg against Dye's 1.10e-6, so the heat of K-40 is 2.2 percent below Dye's table; U and Th are within 0.3 percent). The test sums the molar mass of every stored atmosphere species from its elements; the gas and materials tables of GEN.410 and GEN.427 add their compounds to that check when they land. Finding for the owner of habitability_world.MOLAR_KG: its sulfur dioxide (0.06407) and methane (0.01604) are 1.9e-4 above the element sums (0.064058, 0.016043), from older weights. As built (GEN.410, first slice): physics/gases.py loads physics/data/gases.json, built by scripts/build_gas_table.py from the tables of research parts 01, 02 and 03 (docs/design/composition/atmosphere-composition-parts/): 78 gases and compounds (the 72 of part 01 T1 and the six it adds in section 5), each with formula, name (spelled as the code spells it), CAS number, molar mass computed from physics.elements (isotope masses for 3He, HD, HDO and radon; the research part's own figure is kept and the loader refuses a row more than 0.01 g/mol away), the infrared flag (monatomic none, homonuclear diatomics collision-induced only, the rest infrared), the volatility class, the photolysis threshold and channel, the near-ultraviolet flag, triple, boiling and critical points with the tag of where each came from, the acentric factor, ideal-gas heat capacity at 100, 300 and 1000 K, gamma, latent heats, and for 24 gases the Rayleigh cross section at 450, 550 and 650 nm with the King factor and the reference temperature its refractivity fit was made at (N2, Ar, CO2, CH4, N2O 288.15 K; O2, CO, H2, NH3 273.15 K; none for the polarizability route and for water vapor, whose refractivity has no known reference temperature, so it takes the generic value). CO takes the Allen/Cox form, CH4 the He 2021 dispersion and water vapor the vapor depolarization. Each row has source, state and, when not verified, the limit naming the block the research did not reach (17 rows have a phase-point, heat-capacity or identity block not reached; 13 Rayleigh entries are second-hand, assumed or on the polarizability route, good to -9 to +6 percent). It is a constants table with no weights and no class gate; the recipes of GEN.411 and GEN.454 to GEN.456 cite its rows by formula and add the class gate. The test test_gas_table.py fails when a gas named in docs/design/research/imported/atmospheric-toxicity.md is not a row, or when the text atmosphere.describe writes names a gas that is not a row (GEN.415: no planet class carries an atmosphere text any more, only air, whether it has air; every gas name in the generator's text is the gas table's, atmosphere.gas_name and atmosphere.species_name, one spelling, "water vapor"). Second slice, Henry constants and viscosity: 35 gases carry henry (the NIST WebBook median kH at 298.15 K in mol/(kg bar) with its range and count, the temperature coefficient d, the independent thermo fit for the audit, a dissociates flag for CO2, SO2, NH3, H2S, HCl and HCN whose constant is the physical solubility only, and Henry.kh(T), which refuses a temperature outside 273.15 to 323.15 K where the research gives the law); 38 carry viscosity (Lennard-Jones sigma and epsilon/k, the NIST value at 300 K where one was read, and Gas.viscosity_upa_s(T) by Chapman-Enskog with the Neufeld integral, which refuses kT/epsilon outside 0.3 to 100, and refuses water vapor, which is polar, 10 percent off and has only its NIST 9.77 uPa s at 300 K). The coefficient part 01 printed (26.693) is micropoise: the code uses 2.6693 for uPa s, and a test reproduces all 37 non-polar viscosities of the research table to 0.01. A Henry entry is partly verified when it rests on one compiled value, the compiled values span more than a factor 3, or the NIST median and the thermo fit differ by more than a factor 2; a viscosity is partly verified when no NIST value was read or it is more than 5 percent from it. Third slice, infrared bands and ultraviolet bins: 37 gases carry infrared, the band centers of part 03 table 2.1 in cm-1 (Band.wavelength_um gives the wavelength; a band the research writes as a range, such as the CH3 modes of methanol, has range_cm1 and no center; approximate marks a value the research writes with "about", which it recalled and did not read), the rank of the gas in the [R0620] saturated-absorption ordering where the research states one (H2O 11, CO2 16, CH4 27, C2H2 6, SO2 8, CH3OH 1) and the research's own band and strength cells kept whole, because they carry the forcing statements, overlaps and broadening that are not numbers. A gas with no fundamentals (the noble gases, N2, O2, H2, the metals and oxides, HNO3) has an empty band list and the research's reason in the row; a row of recalled band positions is partly verified with that limit. 22 gases carry uv, the mean total photoabsorption cross section in cm2 per molecule over the 35 bins gases.UV_BINS_NM (10 to 1100 nm, part 02 section 2.3), None where the bin lies outside the data (the source package treats it as zero, and UvAbsorption.at returns 0.0), a flag where under half of the bin is covered, the wavelengths the data cover and where they come from ([R1873] or [R2040] unless the research names another). The research's H2CO is the table's CH2O. The builder checks that every typed band number is in the research cell and that every bin is missing exactly where the coverage says it lies outside the data. The limits the research names are kept on the entry: the structured Schumann-Runge bands of O2 (175 to 200 nm), where a bin mean understates the transmission of a thick column by orders of magnitude and the generator needs a k-distribution or effective cross section, and the visible CH4 bins, which rest on [R2258] paper the research did not reach. UvAbsorption.longest_wavelength_above(x) gives the upper edge of the last bin at or above a cross section. The bin means are inputs for the photolysis and shielding items (GEN.413, GEN.436); nothing reads them yet, so there is no generator epoch. Still to build: the saturation-pressure curves (GEN.452, parts 01 sections 6 and 7, the composite of Wagner, Fray and Schmitt and the laboratory fits) and the class gate (GEN.411).
  2. Gases and materials (GEN.410, GEN.427): the two tables every body reads. The materials table is the one shared components table of section 4a item 8; the gas table is the atmosphere's. Molar masses come from the elements. Every row is a row of the loader (section 4b) with a class gate, a source and a verification state. As built (GEN.427): physics/materials.py loads physics/data/materials.json (158 rows), built by scripts/build_materials_table.py from part E's materials table, part B's SLB phases and iron rows, part C's ice phases and the values read from [R3992: Tables 1 to 3], the Handbook of Mineralogy and PubChem (scripts/materials_data.py). Every number has a source key and part E's label code, and a value with no source is null (the recalled cells of part E are dropped, not kept). Rows are gated any or planetary, equations of state are kept as each source gives them (Vinet, Birch-Murnaghan, SLB Mie-Grueneisen-Debye, the modified polytrope, tables), and the 84 asteroid components, the field families, the comet list and the class composition texts all resolve to rows (a test fails otherwise).   3. Stars (GEN.439, GEN.440): composition is a pure function of the stored [Fe/H], age and population (population from the age gate where a star has none). It adds no column to the 75 million stars. As built (GEN.439): physics/stellar_abundances.py holds the relations of docs/design/composition/stellar-composition.md as pure functions with no draw in them: the [R0168] scale moved to proto-solar (PROTO_MASS_FRACTION, the derived helium-to-metal ratio 1.674, built from the element table's atomic weights), the age gate and the Bensby logistic for the branch (BRANCH_TABLE, a row pair of the GEN.472 loader that registers itself, the high-alpha row first so one fixed uniform number decides it and the branch flips at most once as [Fe/H] falls), the hinge fits of [X/Fe] with the Cholesky-correlated residuals and the carbon-enhanced share (abundance_offsets, which takes the normals and uniforms as arguments so GEN.440 can key them to the star), X, Y, Z and the element fractions (composition), the Fe/Si, Mg/Si, C/O and O/Si ratios, the first dredge-up of Boothroyd and Sackmann, the birth Eu, Th and U for GEN.459, and the remnants: the white dwarf's core type, the neutron star's constants, the black hole's quantities and the brown dwarf's limits and radius (GEN.590 removed the Chandrasekhar white dwarf table and the Chamel and Haensel outer-crust list, which the downloaded Bedard et al. sequences and Servais and Chamel crust files replaced). The fit constants are in tuning.py with their sources; RELATIONS lists every relation with its source and verification state, and the four that stay open or stand-in (neon, the neutron star's inner crust and core, and the brown dwarf's radius and hydrogen limit against metallicity) wait for a source or for an integrated equation of state. As built (GEN.590): physics/stellar_composition.py reads the downloaded files and requires them (Boss's 'Require the data'): a main-sequence star's MIST track and lifetime, a white dwarf's radius and layers at its temperature, a neutron star's outer crust; bulk generation refuses to start without them (gap-filling-plan.md 6.6). Nothing stored changes, so there is no epoch. As built (GEN.440): physics/stellar_composition.py is the derived function. star_makeup(kind, fe_h, age_gy, mass_sol, key) returns the birth and surface composition, the branch, the star's draws and what is particular to the kind (the dredge-up of a red giant, a brown dwarf's limits and radius, a white dwarf's core, atmosphere and radius, a neutron star's outer crust, a black hole's horizon quantities, a Wolf-Rayet star's surface fractions), and names what the design leaves open for that kind. The draws are a stream of their own, abundance:{key}, drawn in a fixed order and all always drawn, so the same star has the same composition at any worker count, an edited [Fe/H], age or mass re-derives it, and a group's members (a system's primary, a cluster's seed) share the branch and offsets and take their own phase. kind_of finds the kind from the stored Yerkes class (a Wolf-Rayet star's "W" included, GEN.831: its subtype "WN6", "WC4" or "WO2" picks the row of tuning.STELLAR_WOLF_RAYET), the phase and the mass. No column, no stored value and no edit to evolve_star: the test sweeps the kinds evolve_star makes and checks each against the relations.
  4. Bodies (GEN.428 to GEN.430, GEN.441, GEN.442, GEN.457 to GEN.466): the bulk fractions, the surface and the small bodies. The formation distance, the snow line and the star's abundances decide the gate. As built (GEN.458): formation.snow_line_au(luminosity) is the 182 K blackbody position (condensate_shares.ICE_CONDENSATION_K, [R2726]; 2.34 AU for the Sun since GEN.950, 2.68 AU at the 170 K it first used; the fixed 2.7 AU constant is gone) and formation.draw_snow_line_au draws a star's own line around it, 0.2 dex in log10 cut at 3 sigma (tuning.SNOW_LINE_SCATTER_DEX, SNOW_LINE_SCATTER_MAX_SIGMA), from a stream of its own (snow_line:<mass>:<age>:<galactic distance>) so no other draw moves. Every star and compact remnant stores it (Star.snow_line_au, stars.snow_line_km, schema v132); a close pair's merged proxy takes the square root of the sum of the squares of its stars' lines, derived from the stored pair and not stored. The disk walk that counts planets and the disk's outer edge read the stored line. Planet.born_beyond_snow_line (planets and moons columns) is distance >= the host's line, set once the orbits are final, a moon taking its planet's; belts and comets read the stored line when GEN.442 lands.   5. Atmospheres (GEN.411, GEN.412, GEN.454 to GEN.456): partial pressures first, then the molar mass, the pressure and the scale height from them. As built (GEN.478): physics/atmosphere_recipes.py loads physics/data/atmosphere_recipes.json, built by scripts/build_atmosphere_recipes.py from part 09 of the atmosphere research (its words) and scripts/atmosphere_recipes_data.py (the machine columns): the 104 rows of its 16 categories as the tables atmosphere-a to atmosphere-p of the loader, each row with its class gate, conditions on stored quantities, a weight expression, its source with the verification label, its source state and, for an open row, the research item that replaces it (R1 to R15 are GEN.480 to GEN.494). The rows that compete for 100 percent are 14 groups (a5 to a7, b1 to b4, c1 to c4, c5 and c6, d1 to d5, e1 to e4, f1 to f4, g, h, i, j, k, l, p); a group is drawn once, a level-2 row is gated on drawn.<group> so the joint probability is parent times conditional, and where every weight is zero the group's stored fallback row wins (c6, d1, e1, l2, or the explicit "none" of j and k). The other rows are presence probabilities (trace gases, clouds by temperature), continuous quantities as a parametric draw and as a binned table (normal, log-normal, uniform, log-uniform or a mixture), derived values, the cloud windows a profile crosses and one choice (the rotation track). A DESIGN bracket is a seeded constant drawn in the file's order (or the bracket's midpoint where nothing was drawn), never a fixed number. The expressions are Python arithmetic over named stored quantities (INPUTS lists them, with their units) and a few functions; the loader parses them with ast and refuses anything else, a name it does not know, a circle of w() or p() references, a probability above 1, a class it does not know, or a row without a source and a state. The corrections of the research are in: the shoreline of d is the [R0364] logistic (the K window fitted to 13 Solar System bodies is gone and the four atmosphere regimes carry its probability by design edges), a3 and a4 follow the 22 polluted white dwarfs, f7 has the explicit core (center 0.57, stopped at 0.8) and the planet tail of 0.043 to 0.087 (part 05's recipe put 13.9 percent of giants above C/O 0.8), the lava-world boundaries are the text edges (the caption list differs by 63 K), p4 takes the saturation time from the stellar mass, and j2 to j4 are outputs of the retention physics (Boss, fork 7), so 8 rows are SOURCED, 51 DERIVED and 45 DESIGN where the part had 8, 48 and 48. Of the 43 open rows two are stand-ins (a2, the law fitted to the Moon, Mars and Earth, and k7, one comet's species list). Nothing reads the tables yet, so no draw changes and there is no epoch of its own: GEN.411, GEN.413, GEN.414, GEN.451 and GEN.454 to GEN.456 take the one bump of the chain. As built (GEN.495): physics/comet_population.py loads physics/data/comet_population.json and draws a comet's coma as percent of H2O: the carbon oxides first, S = (CO + CO2)/H2O log-normal (median 18 percent, 0.2 dex since GEN.629) and f = CO/(CO + CO2) logit-normal around the class median (0.08 Jupiter-family and Halley-type, 0.5 long-period, logit scatter 1.5), CO = S f and CO2 = S (1 - f), redrawn together while either lies outside the observed bounds (CO 0.15 to 30, CO2 2 to 37; at most 1000 tries, then PopulationError, never a clipped value); then CH4, C2H6, NH3, HCN, CH3OH, H2CO and H2S as independent truncated log-normals with the geometric mean and scatter of part G1 section 2.3, one random number each by inverse transform; then the carbon-chain-depleted flag from the registered table comet-carbon-chain (a pair of gated rows per period class: 0.37, 0.25, 0.185). An interstellar comet draws CO/H2O log-uniform in 0.01 to 2 and CO2/H2O log-uniform in 0.02 to 10 (the object measured at 3.32 AU is far outside the Solar System range) and the other species from the population. Each number has its source, label and state in the file; 8 of the 9 species rows are verified; the partly verified ones (H2S from nine comets, the 0.2 dex width of S, the class medians of f, the flag fractions with the Halley-type one, the interstellar edges) name their limit. 67P, 103P/Hartley 2 and Hale-Bopp are test targets only (in test_comet_population.py: inside the bounds and where the draw has density), never a center. The pooled CO and CO2 over the three classes have geometric means 2.5 and 12.4 percent and scatter 0.54 and 0.22 dex against the table's 2.6 and 11 and 0.64 and 0.27. GEN.629: the first build gave S a width of 0.1 dex, the +/- 4 of HP22's median converted, which is narrow against the spread of single comets (67P's bulk 7.8 percent was 3.6 sigma below the median). The width is now 0.2 dex, computed from the [R3782] database (df_comp_all.csv, fetched 2026-10-23): the 9 comets with both CO and CO2 measured within 2.5 AU give 0.197 dex (their own median is 15 percent), the 8 within 2.0 AU give 0.138; the widest is kept (Boss's rule for sources in question), the table's median 18 stays, and 67P lies at 1.8 sigma. Nothing stored yet reads the draw (GEN.442, GEN.455, GEN.457 take it), so there is no generator epoch. It supersedes the comet rows k4 to k7 of atmosphere_recipes.
  6. Derived values (GEN.413 to GEN.419, GEN.422, GEN.423, GEN.443 to GEN.446): the colour, the radiation, habitability and the text. Each is a pure function of stored values and the tables.

7.2 One set of units and one source field

  • Storage is SI: kg, m, s, K, Pa, mol; kg/mol for molar mass (the atm_molar_density column keeps its name and unit, decided in the atmosphere note), and the units the existing columns already use stay. Doses are effective dose in mSv per year.
  • A solid or a star is described by mass fractions (X, Y, Z for a star; component fractions of the shared materials table for a body); a gas by partial pressures (the mole fractions follow). Conversion between them uses the element table's molar masses and nothing else.
  • Every number that comes from outside the repository sits in a table row or a named constant that carries its source and its verification state (verified, partly verified with the limit the check named, open with the seeded range used meanwhile, or stand-in with its replacing item). A number in a note has the same marks. The loader refuses a table row without them. The licence is not a column of any table (Boss, 2026-10-22): it is recorded in DATA-LICENSE.md in the repository root, and Boss manages it. Boss ruled at 2:35 PM EDT (GEN.500 removed) that every paper and dataset he acquired was obtained legally and is usable: the raw papers and downloaded datasets stay out of the public repository and are never redistributed or sold, the research results and derived values are fine to publish, and the program stays free forever.
  • Where sources disagree the rules of section 4a item 12 decide, and each design note lists every case with the rule applied.

7.3 Drawn first, or the composition is the cause

Boss (11:58 PM EDT on 2026-10-16): either works, the design picks the simplest consistent one per kind of body. The choice, as the four notes make it:

Kind Composition is Density, radius, gravity

Remnant (white dwarf, neutron star, black hole) derived from the stored mass and the progenitor relation; a black hole carries none the white-dwarf radius is integrated; the neutron-star radius and the main-sequence lifetime are derived (GEN.463, GEN.467)

7.4 Stored against derived

The four notes each hold their table. The rule they share: store a value only when it was drawn, cannot be derived, or is expensive and exact to recompute; derive everything else on demand and test that a cache equals the recomputation. A recalculation after an orbit validation or a zone change never redraws a stored composition (finding F10).

7.5 The generator epoch and the migrations

The designs and the loader change no stored value, so they bump no epoch. The build items do, in the groups the notes name:

  • 8.1: the loader (GEN.472) and the data tables (GEN.410, GEN.427, GEN.438, GEN.439, GEN.440) are building blocks with no behaviour change. GEN.463 and GEN.467 keep their own epochs, joined to the one rescatter. The epoch scope of GEN.427 is Decided (Boss, 2:10 PM EDT, card "Data table, no bump", relayed in words): it is a data table that bumps nothing, and the one bump is GEN.428 to GEN.430 plus DB.69 in 8.4 phase 1.
  • 8.4: one bump for the bulk chain (GEN.428 to GEN.430, DB.69 and the formation items GEN.458 to GEN.460, with the table versions); one for the atmosphere values (GEN.411, GEN.422, GEN.423, GEN.424, GEN.452, GEN.454, GEN.455 with DB.67 and DB.71); one for the added stored values (GEN.413, GEN.414, GEN.456 with DB.68). The revisions take the next free numbers at build time.
  • 9.0: the colour functions (GEN.416 to GEN.419) are pure functions of stored values and need no migration. The radiation items (GEN.443, GEN.444) replace the dose proxies with a bump and a migration of the dose columns, after GEN.453 has corrected the constants.

A change to a table row or weight is a change of that table's version and an epoch bump (section 4b); Registry.versions pins each table's version and digest.
