3b. Composition as weighted probability tables (Boss, 1:40 AM EDT)

Boss's requirement: every composition category has a table of possibilities with weighted probabilities; every row lists the planet or object classes it supports (the gate); the generator draws among the rows that pass the gate with weights normalised to 100 percent. Part 09 restates every recipe of parts 05, 06 and 07 (plus escape and UV from 04) in that form. Design points:

  • Row: id, option, gate, weight (a number, or a function of mass, temperature, pressure, redox, insolation, host star, formation distance a_form over the snow line), weight source with verification label, status, notes. Status: SOURCED (numbers from a read source), DERIVED (computed from a stated formula), DESIGN Rn (no sourced weight; the physical bracket is given and the generator draws a seeded constant inside it meanwhile, nothing fixed).
  • Combining: W_i = gate_i x weight_i; p_i = W_i / sum W. Edge clauses can be softened with a normal CDF of stated width. If the sum is zero the table's named fallback row (bare, airless, absent) wins. Level-2 tables draw only after their parent row (joint probability = parent x conditional). Draw order: host star, then the 16 category tables, with the last four reading earlier draws. One seeded random wrapper (GEN.56) is the only random source.
  • Continuous quantities (redox, metallicity, C/O) are given both as a parametric draw and as a binned table so either can be coded.
  • Size: 16 categories (a mantle redox, b volatile inventory and water fraction by snow line, c outgassed regime, d retention regime, e sub-Neptune envelope, f giants, g brown dwarfs, h hot Jupiters, i lava worlds, j frost and evolved atmospheres, k comets and KBOs, l white dwarf and pulsar planets, m trace gases, n clouds, hazes and dust, o ground radioisotopes, p host-star UV regime) and 104 rows: 8 SOURCED, 48 DERIVED, 48 DESIGN. The 48 DESIGN rows group into 15 research needs (R1 to R15, section 7 of this file and part 09 section 4): the main ones are volatile delivery against formation distance (R2), the radius-valley occurrence weights (R1), giant C/O (R5), comet class fractions (R9), trace gases (R11) and brown-dwarf types against temperature (R15).
  • Conflicts the tables exposed (part 09 section 5, 13 items), most relevant to code: the repo has 20 classes (A B C D E F G H I J K L M N O P Q S T V) and no brown-dwarf letter; C, D, S have no atmosphere and no entry in CLASS_MIXES (a pressure would fall back to an N2-O2 mix); free O2 sits in the mixes of G, H, L, O, P, Q, V with no abiotic or life rule and L has 60 percent Ar, far above the radiogenic budget; E and F carry NH3 and CH4 at 340 to 400 K, which photolysis removes; N says "reducing" but its mix is oxidized; I, J, T share one CH4/H2O/H2S mix in every zone; classes I and T cover the same sizes and V straddles the radius valley. In part 05 itself: the C/O recipe puts 13.9 percent of giants above C/O 0.8 where about 2 to 5 percent was intended (the table truncates the core at 0.8 and adds an explicit tail), the lava-world boundary difference is 63 K, not 130 K, and the brown-dwarf range 500 to 700 K is uncovered (row g4 added).

11. Usability rule (Boss, 3:02 AM EDT)

Everything verified or partly verified is usable as long as it is not contradicted; when two sources contradict, the verified one wins. Extended at 12:39 PM EDT: when in doubt, verified wins; when two verified sources are in doubt, the newest wins; when two sources are both in question, the one that takes the most permission wins (his words are 'most permission take wins'; Boss confirmed by card at 1:12 PM EDT on 2026-10-17, relayed in words by the coordinator, that it means the widest range of the two sources). Boss answered a card at 12:40 PM EDT (relayed in words): his six earlier 'widen and keep both' answers stand (arm pitch against bulge fraction, nuclear cluster mass slope, blue to red cluster radius ratio, thick-to-thin height ratio, thick-to-thin mass ratio against Vc, cluster system radius), and the newest-wins rule applies to every conflict that comes after them. Applied to the labels in the parts and the verdicts above:

Label or verdict Usable in a table or formula Limit
READ, DATA, COMPUTED yes wins every contradiction
2ND-HAND yes loses to a READ, DATA or COMPUTED value that disagrees; keep the stated spread
PARTLY (verdict) yes only within the stated limit or corrected value (for example Mars omega0 by wavelength band, lab ice curves inside their measured windows)
CONFIRMED (verdict) yes
RECALLED, SNIPPET only as a flagged placeholder with a seeded range loses to any verified value
UNSUPPORTED, NOT REACHED not as a number; a DESIGN row with a physical bracket instead
CONTRADICTED no use the corrected value given in the verdict
