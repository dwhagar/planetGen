Decided

  • Boss, 6:39 PM EDT 2026-10-23, pipeline card 1, option 1: the planet mass is the isolation mass at the formation distance and the disk's solids, with a scatter and the giant core-accretion rule, tuned against the observed occurrence by the GEN.503 harness (builders GEN.509 and GEN.510). The isolation law is checked above a few Earth masses. The class mass ranges of bulk-composition.md decision 28 are not kept as a hidden stand-in beyond the spine's class-first epoch.

The law (planet_mass.planet_mass(distance_au, star_mass_kg, snow_line_au, fe_h, stream))

  1. Solids. The isolation mass at the formation distance, formation.isolation_mass_kg, for a feeding zone OLIGARCH_SEPARATION_MEDIAN (15.07) mutual Hill radii wide (drawn per system, OLIGARCH_SEPARATION_SIGMA_LN), from the minimum-mass nebula scaled by the star's mass and 10^[Fe/H] (disk_surface_density_scale), the disk's own scatter (DISK_SOLIDS_SCATTER_DEX, 0.7 dex, [R0114]) and boosted by disk_condensation.SNOW_LINE_SOLID_JUMP (1.967 for the Sun: all the solids that stand at the water and ammonia ice lines, the rock, the metal, the refractory carbon and the ices, over the rock alone; GEN.1396 replaced the water-only 2.17 of GEN.950, which counted the ice but left out the ammonia, the carbon and the grains that rise with it) beyond the star's own snow line [R2430, R2709], times PLANET_DISK_SOLIDS_ENHANCEMENT (0.4647, stand-in PA1: the solids against the minimum-mass nebula, tuned to the cold-giant occurrence), and inside 1 AU times (distance / 1 AU) ^ -PLANET_INNER_DISK_STEEPENING (0.75, stand-in PM2). Beyond PLANET_OUTER_GROWTH_AU (11.36 AU) the slow outer disk has built less: the mass falls as the distance to the power PLANET_OUTER_GROWTH_EXPONENT (2.889, stand-in PA1). The isolation formula is built and unchanged.
  2. Assembly. Giant impacts merge a feeding zone's oligarchs; that is the architecture's thinning (planet_architecture.sculpt, Pu and Wu 2015), not a constant survival share, so the old GIANT_IMPACT_SURVIVAL_FRACTION (0.4) and PLANET_ASSEMBLY_SCATTER_DEX are gone. A planet asked for alone is one oligarch of its feeding zone.
  3. Runaway. A core of GIANT_CORE_CRITICAL_MASS_EARTH = 10 Earth masses or more, beyond the snow line, becomes a gas giant with probability GIANT_GAS_SURVIVAL_SHARE (0.766, stand-in PA2) times 10^(GIANT_GAS_SURVIVAL_METALLICITY_EXPONENT [Fe/H]) (0.6498, PA2) times the occurrence factor for the star's [Fe/H] (metallicity.giant_occurrence_factor, the existing 10^(2 [Fe/H]) law, capped at 1), times (GIANT_GAS_SURVIVAL_CUT_AU / distance)^2 beyond GIANT_GAS_SURVIVAL_CUT_AU (9.022 AU, PA2) (the outer cores grow too slowly to reach the critical mass before the gas is gone). The giant's mass follows dN/dlogM ~ M^-0.31 ([R0960], the slope already in GIANT_MASS_FUNCTION_SLOPE) between GIANT_RUNAWAY_MIN_MASS_EARTH = 30 and the lighter of 13 Jupiter masses and 1% of the star. A core that finds no gas stays a solid or ice-giant core, at most GIANT_NO_GAS_CORE_MAX_EARTH = 20 Earth masses (the envelope share of such a body is the composition lane's draw, GEN.428).
  4. Cap. No planet's solids exceed PLANET_MAX_STAR_MASS_RATIO (1%, the existing GIANT_MAX_STAR_MASS_RATIO) of its star.

Draw order on the body's stream when a planet is asked for alone: the disk scatter and the spacing (draw_disk_scatter, draw_separation), then (only for a core past the critical mass beyond the line) the gas-survival draw, then the runaway mass. The same stream gives the same planet.

Not in this law

  • Moons are separate (GEN.466, parent and the circumplanetary ice line).
  • The formation distance. The law takes it as an argument. Today a planet's slot distance is its formation distance. The migrant draw (a giant formed beyond the line now sitting close in, the "born beyond the snow line or migrant" logistic of GEN.510) supplies a different formation distance when it lands; until then there are no hot giants, which is the main difference from today's mix (below).
  • Radius, density, envelope. Derived from the mass and composition by the spine (GEN.428, GEN.429), not drawn here.
  • Belts and small bodies keep their own laws (GEN.442).

What changes against today's class-first masses

Measured with 400 generated systems (3,205 planets, seed 11) by the sweep in the lane's report reports/planet-mass-law-sweep-2026-10-24.md: today the mass is a class draw with almost no link to the distance. The law is physical, so it differs where the class draw was not:

  • Giants (30 Earth masses or more): 13.7% of planets today, 8.4% under the law. Inside the snow line today 5 to 14% of planets are giants; under the law under 1% (no migrants yet). Beyond the line 14 to 22% today, 9 to 21% under the law, rising with distance.
  • Median mass: about 0.05 to 0.13 Earth masses at every distance today; the law gives 0.004 to 0.02 inside the snow line and 0.2 to 1.3 beyond, rising with distance (dim red-dwarf disks are light), and a wider spread (p10 to p90 spans four to five orders of magnitude against three today).
  • This is explained, not hidden: the law is the intended physics and the numbers are stand-ins. The three knobs that move the giant share and the median are PLANET_DISK_SOLIDS_ENHANCEMENT, PLANET_ASSEMBLY_SCATTER_DEX and GIANT_GAS_SURVIVAL_SHARE; the migrant draw (GEN.510) restores close-in giants. Tuning them against the observed occurrence is the harness's job (GEN.503, GEN.509, GEN.510); the stand-ins were chosen by hand so that the giant share is the same order as today's (8.4% against 13.7%) and the median rises with distance.

Tuned against the occurrence of planets (2026-10-28, GEN.711, Aux 16's check)

Aux 16's distribution check (reports/planet-class-distribution-check-2026-10-25.md) found the class draw far off the observed occurrence: about 50 times too many hot Jupiters around M dwarfs, about 10 times too many giants around M dwarfs, a missing 2 to 20 Earth-mass population. The first version of this law (enhancement 5, survival 0.5, no cut) fixed the hot Jupiters but gave 1.2 giants of 100 Earth masses or more per Sun-like star and far too few super-Earths around M dwarfs.
