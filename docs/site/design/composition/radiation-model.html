3. Transmission by band from the stored mixture

Column and optical depths: X = 0.1 P / g in g/cm2; mean molar mass mu = sum x_i M_i; N_i = x_i X N_A / mu in cm-2. Rayleigh tau_R(lambda) = N sum x_i sigma_i, with sigma_i from part 02 table 2.1 (verified for 13 gases) or the generic 1.3057e-28 alpha^2 F_K lambda^-4 (1 + B/lambda^2)^2 (mean ratio 0.988, sd 0.046; 1.9 percent low on Earth air). Absorption tau_abs = sum N_j sigma_j(lambda, T) from part 02 2.3 and 2.4 (O3 column from GEN.413).

Direct beam F cos(chi) exp(-m tau), m the Chapman function of R/H. Thick air and the diffuse field use the Eddington transmission T(tau, mu0) = [(2/3 + mu0) + (2/3 - mu0) exp(-tau/mu0)] / (4/3 + tau), R = 1 - T, spherical albedo s = integral of 2 mu (1 - T), and with ground albedo A the reflectance R + T A (1 - s)/(1 - A s) and ground flux T/(1 - A s) (part 02 3.5). Band transmission is the star-weighted mean of T(lambda) over the band edges of R6.

Aerosols and cloud add tau_aer and (1 - g) tau_c to the two-stream, or use the Monte Carlo reflectance table (g 0.85, omega 1, mu0 0.5: 0.462 at tau 5, 0.870 at tau 50; omega 0.99 saturates near 0.60). Dust tau is a drawn clear-to-storm column; haze and cloud masses come from GEN.414.

The star's side: the Sun at 1 au gives 6.85, 17.44 and 86.00 W/m2 in UV-C (200 to 280), UV-B and UV-A, an erythemally weighted 666 and a Setlow-weighted 8.66 W/m2 (ATLAS-1, part 02 2.7). Other stars need a UV source (R17).

4. Greenhouse from the infrared opacity, and the check of the GEN.412 multiplier

The repo variable is G = (T_s/T_e)^4 - 1 with T_s^4 = (1 - A) S (1 + G) / (4 sigma). The closure of [R3783] (part 03 5.3, 5.4, verified) has tau(p) = tau0 (p/p0)^n, diffusivity D = 1.66, lapse scaling alpha, beta = alpha (gamma - 1)/gamma, and T0^4 = T_e^4 (1 + D tau0 / 2) when purely radiative; otherwise tau_rc and T0 follow from the continuity of flux and temperature. The tropopause obeys p_tp proportional to tau0^(-1/n).

tau0 from composition: CIA by the Planck-weighted pair coefficients of part 03 3.1 (computable), water self-continuum from MT_CKD (exponent 5 to 6 in the window, the origin of the runaway), lines from the correlated-k grids of R2. The Koll equivalent grey thickness of CIA alone is a lower bound. Forcing laws [R0620, R1302] apply only as perturbations of an Earth-like state; Myhre 5.35 ln(C/C0) fails above 1 bar of CO2. [R3229: Table 3] (read): CO2 5.35 and N2O 0.12 are Myhre's own values; IPCC 1990 gave 6.3 and 0.14, so the 'IPCC 1990' labels in part 03 should read [R3229]. Myhre's CO2 doubling is 3.71 W/m2; its Table 2 total (2.247) does not equal its rows (2.2537). Runaway limits: 282 (Simpson-Nakajima), 291 (Kopparapu) and 310 W/m2 (Kasting); a stored T_s above the limit with liquid water is rejected or made a steam atmosphere (part 03 6.3). A haze that absorbs sunlight and passes infrared cools the surface (Titan, over 20 K); that needs the two-channel form (Titan tau0 comes out 3.5 times low in one channel).

The check of a stored multiplier, in order: (1) from stored A, S, T_s compute T_e and G; (2) invert the closure for tau0 implied (monotone, bisection); (3) compute tau0 from composition; (4) hard test: tau0 implied is not below the CIA floor; soft test: ratio to the composition value within a factor set when the grids exist (open); (5) when the grids exist T_s is derived and G becomes the readout. The doubling test: a CO2 forcing of 5.32 ln 2 = 3.69 W/m2 (the printed + 0.39 term of part 03 2.2 is probably a squared-log term lost in extraction and is not used) needs tau0 raised by 5.0 percent and warms Earth's state by 1.12 K at fixed absorbed flux (section 11). Myhre's 5.35 ln 2 = 3.71 W/m2 gives 5.1 percent and 1.13 K; the two laws differ by 0.5 percent, so the test takes 3.7 W/m2 (Rule applied: newest, [R0620], drives the computed shift; [R3229] is the check).

5. Shielding by column and its composition

Proton cutoff. The kinetic energy that crosses X g/cm2 vertically comes from inverting the PSTAR range (part 07 2.5, verified): air 2241 MeV at 1033 g/cm2, 420 at 100, 171 at 22.5; CO2 165 MeV at 21 g/cm2 (247 MeV at 60 degrees, 10B 2.1); hydrogen needs 1.65 times, helium 1.06, water 1.07, rock 0.96 (ratios at 22.5 g/cm2). Mixtures use the Bragg sum of mass stopping powers. Slant paths and the nuclear loss exp(-X / lambda_I) (lambda_I 90.1 for air, 88.9 CO2, 52.0 H2, PDG) apply to primaries. Above about 100 g/cm2 the dose is cascade secondaries, not primaries.

Galactic cosmic rays. Dipole cutoff Rc = 14.34 GV (M/M_E)(R_E/r)^2 cos^4(lat) (scaling M/r^2, replaces (R_E/r)^3; partly verified: textbook form, constants computed). D0(X) is the Atri table at M = 0 (553, 263, 17.2, 3.02, 0.65 mSv/yr at 100, 200, 500, 700, 1036 g/cm2), f_B the Griessmeier ratios (M = 1: 0.31 at X = 100, 0.52 at 1036; valid X of 100 or more and M up to 10), proton primaries only, so a lower bound. Composition enters through X_eff = sum X_k (90.1 / lambda_k) (body part E 2.9d; ground gram = 0.89 air, ice 1.08). Neutron share: 2 to 7 percent of absorbed dose; secondary neutrons dominate only the dose equivalent (Mars about 50 percent at the surface, over 80 percent below 50 cm; absorbed share 2 to 9 percent; 08B 4.3, 10B 2.3).

Stellar particles. J(>R) = J0 R^-g1 exp(-R/R0) for R up to R1 = (g2 - g1) R0, and J0 R1^-g1 exp(-R1/R0) (R/R1)^-g2 above (continuous). The event of 2006-12-13 has J0 1.33e8, g1 1.045, g2 5.80, R0 0.1765 GV; the 1976 event has 7.62e6, 1.710, 6.33, 0.2054 (10B 1.2a). Transmitted share = the fluence above max(R_atm, Rc) over the >30 MeV fluence. Photons and gamma: mass attenuation by sum w_i (mu/rho)_i (part 07 2.4).

Dose quantities. Absorbed (Gy), equivalent (Sv, wR) and effective (Sv, wT). ICRP 103 wR (secondary source, R3): photons, electrons, muons 1; protons and charged pions 2; alpha particles, fission fragments and heavy ions 20; neutrons 2.5 + 18.2 exp(-[ln E]^2 / 6) below 1 MeV, 5.0 + 17.0 exp(-[ln 2E]^2 / 6) from 1 to 50, 2.5 + 3.25 exp(-[ln 0.04E]^2 / 6) above (peak 20.7 at 1 MeV, floor 2.5; Boss's 5 to 20 is corrected). wT (sum 1.00) belongs to tissues, not particles: marrow, colon, lung, stomach, breast, remainder 0.12; gonads 0.08; thyroid, oesophagus, liver, bladder 0.04; bone surface, skin, brain, salivary glands 0.01. The Moon anchor of 500 mSv/yr against 80 to 99 mGy/yr absorbed implies Q near 5.5. [R1843: Tables 1 and 3] (read, GCR only, about 300 sols, column about 21 g/cm2): 0.21 +- 0.04 mGy/day, mean Q 3.05 +- 0.26, 0.64 +- 0.12 mSv/day, 76 mGy/yr and 232 mSv/yr; MSL cruise 0.48 mGy/day, Q 3.82, 1.84 mSv/day. Hassler uses the ICRP 60 function Q(L) (Q = 1 below L = 10 keV/micron, 0.32 L - 2.2 from 10 to 100, 300/sqrt(L) above 100; also in the ICRP extract), not ICRP 103 weighting factors, so the Mars and Moon anchors are dose equivalents, not effective doses, and the stored quantity (R12) differs from them by a definition. The paper's SEP sentence (about 25 percent of the GCR dose equivalent) holds for absorbed dose only (24 percent; 8 percent for dose equivalent); one SEP event is 0.025 mGy in Table 2 and about 50 microGy in the text (unexplained); the mission total is 982 mSv from the tables against about 1.01 Sv in the text. W(air) = 33.97 eV (ICRU 90) for ionisation rates.

Muons. Crouch fit I(h) = exp(-11.22 - 0.00262 h) + exp(-14.10 - 0.001213 h) + 2.17e-13 (h in hg/cm2 = m water equivalent), valid from about 1 km w.e.; dose = pi I x 2 MeV cm2/g. Below that it is an extrapolation (Titan's 10850 g/cm2 gives 3.4e-7 Gy/yr against 3.2e-6, a factor 9 low).

6. The ground: radiogenic dose and radon

Gamma dose in air at 1 m: D = 5.70 U + 2.44 Th + 13.1 K nGy/h (U and Th in ppm, K in wt percent; from UNSCEAR 0.462, 0.604, 0.0417 nGy/h per Bq/kg and 12.35, 4.04 Bq/kg per ppm, 314 Bq/kg per wt percent; verified, part 07 2.10). Effective annual dose = D x 8766 h x 0.7 Sv/Gy x occupancy. Age: scale each isotope by exp(-lambda (t - 4.567 Gyr)) with half-lives K-40 1.262e9, U-238 4.4683e9, Th-232 1.41e10 yr (part 07 2.9). Under dense gas multiply by exp(-0.0637 rho h) (rho in g/cm3, h = 100 cm); the Earth coefficients are valid for rho below about 0.03 g/cm3 (F9).

Radon: J = 12.35 U f rho_b sqrt(lambda D_e) (f 0.2, rho_b 1600 kg/m3 recovered from the 0.026 of part 07 2.10, D_e 2e-6 m2/s), C = J tau / h with tau 5.52 d, dose 9 nSv per Bq h m-3 times equilibrium factor 0.6 outdoors. Heat uses the same abundances: h = rho (3.387e-3 K + 26.18 Th + 98.29 U) microW/m3 (kg/kg), so the dose and the radiogenic flux of GEN.459 cannot drift apart (primitive mantle with the 2003 edition values below: 21.0 TW for 4.042e24 kg; the older 20 ppb U, 77 ppb Th, 280 ppm K of part 07 2.9 gave 19.95 TW with Rb; Rule applied: verified). Reference abundances: Palme and O'Neill 2003 edition Table 4, K 260 ppm, Th 83.4 ppb, U 21.8 ppb (read twice); the '[R3417]' Th 84.9 and U 22.9 ppb of our files (1.8 and 5.0 percent higher) cannot be confirmed from a paper and are not used (Rule applied: verified; newest cannot apply because the 2014 edition is not in hand). The primitive-mantle oxides sum to 98.41 weight percent, not 100 (1.34 minor oxides, under 0.15 trace), so a normalisation to 100 must say so. Crust: [R3842: Table 11] (the 2003 text), upper crust Th 10.5, U 2.7 ppm, K2O 2.80 weight percent (K 23,244 ppm); total crust 5.6, 1.3 and 15,026 ppm; every crust value in our files matches it, and Palme and O'Neill's own crust column (Th 3.5, U 0.91 ppm, K 9100 ppm) disagrees (Rule applied: verified, the dedicated crustal review). A rocky surface is crust, not mantle: the primitive mantle gives 0.67 nGy/h, the upper crust 71.5 and the UNSCEAR soil 59.9, so worlds B, C, D carry the primitive mantle as the low bracket and B also the upper-crust value. Ground type and band albedo come from GEN.441 (lab reflectance, not Bond albedo; the conversion is open). An airless ice moon has almost no K, U, Th and so almost no ground dose.

7. Surface ultraviolet from transmission and reflectance

E(lambda) at the ground = direct + diffuse + ground-reflected, from section 3. UVI = 40 m2/W x integral S_er E dlambda, with the CIE erythemal S_er = 1 for 250 to 298 nm, 10^(0.094 (298 - lambda)) to 328 nm, 10^(0.015 (140 - lambda)) to 400 nm (partly verified, the NOAA text has a typo). DNA dose = integral S_Setlow (lambda/300) E dlambda (energy basis; the file says unity at 300 nm but stores 0.0333, reported not resolved). The direct UVI falls as DU^-1.08 for 100 to 500 DU. A planet with O2 but no ozone has lethal UV-C (Setlow dose about 280 times the 300 DU case). Ground reflectance enters through 1/(1 - A s), an upper bound because the return path is not ozone-filtered.
