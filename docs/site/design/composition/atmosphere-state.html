The air's density, scale height and greenhouse warming from its mixture (GEN.423)

docs/design/composition/atmosphere-state.md

Module: src/planetgen/physics/atmosphere_state.py. Tests: src/tests/test_atmosphere_state.py. Lane L, 2026-10-24.

What it does

Given the mixture (the dict of physics/mixture.py: gas-table formula to partial pressure in Pa), a temperature and a gravity:

Quantity Formula
mean molar mass M = sum x_i M_i
surface density rho = P M / (R T)
scale height H = R T / (M g) (so rho g H = P)
column mass P / g
mass of gas i above 1 m2 m_i = x_i M_i P / (M g) (they add up to P / g)
warming T_s from the greenhouse table (physics/greenhouse_table.py, GEN.850, design note greenhouse-table.md): the surface temperature the radiative-convective model gives for the mixture, the gravity and the absorbed flux (and a haze's share of it taken aloft); G = (T_s/T_e)^4 - 1

A change of the mix therefore moves all of them: swapping N2 for CO2 raises M and rho and lowers H; adding water vapor raises G.

Limits

The warming is the fitted table of physics/radiative_convective.py (GEN.850), which reads the downloaded ExoMolOP and HITRAN line data (GEN.715): its accuracy, range and stand-ins are those of greenhouse-table.md and line-opacity.md. The stored water vapor is the surface's, thinned with height by Manabe and Wetherald's humidity profile. The grey closure of GEN.703 (infrared_opacity.py, Robinson and Catling's closure in greenhouse.py) is gone.

Wiring

surface_air.solve calls greenhouse_warming at each step of the fixed point (GEN.850), with the haze's share of the sunlight and its level when the air hazes (GEN.851); physics/planets.py stores the answer.
