F2 Melt iron redox to dIW

[R4154: Eq. 4] (peridotite liquid, 1 bar, 2173 K, 11 experiments, dIW -1.5 to +6.4): log10(X_FeO1.5 / X_FeO) = (0.252 +/- 0.015) dIW - (1.530 +/- 0.053). Fe3+/Fe-total measured 0.014 +/- 0.009 at dIW -1.5 to 0.440 +/- 0.008 at +6.4. Inverse (COMPUTED, G3_fe3_to_diw.py): dIW = (log10(f/(1-f)) + 1.530)/0.252.

Fe3+/Fe_total 0.014 0.02 0.037 0.05 0.10 0.20 0.44
dIW (Sossi scale) -1.26 -0.64 +0.45 +1.00 +2.28 +3.68 +5.66

So a stored mantle Fe3+/Fe-total (a part-B quantity) gives dIW directly, with the stoichiometric slope (about 1/4) and one decade of Fe3+/Fe2+ per 4 dIW units. Magma-ocean Fe3+/Fe-total from metal-silicate equilibration: Earth 0.034 to 0.10 (28 to 53 GPa, 2300 to 4100 K), Mars 0.026 to 0.038 ([R1951]:221, READ abstract); these map to dIW +0.3 to +2.3 and -0.2 to +0.5 (COMPUTED, indicative, pressure outside calibration).

F4 Mass action rule for a generator (2 reservoirs)

Per volatile element e: bulk mass = melt mass x dissolved fraction + atmosphere mass; atmosphere mass of species s = 4 pi R^2 p_s mu_s / (g mu_atm) (Lichtenberg Eq. 10; Maurice Appendix C). Gas speciation: reactions CO/CO2, H2/H2O, CO + 3H2 = CH4 + H2O from F3 at the surface T, with fO2 = IW(T,P) + stored dIW. Dissolved amounts: Henry-type laws F5 per species. Solve for p_s with mass balance ([R2957, R4154] do this; Maurice reports surface dIW varies by about 3 log units over mass and magma-ocean-fraction space for each fixed dIW at the magma-ocean bottom).

F7 Lava-world vapour (dry melt, no H, C, N, S)

Use reaction-equilibrium vapour pressures, not an on-the-fly Gibbs minimiser: log10 P_i = [sum nu_ij mu_j + nu_O2 mu0_O2 - mu0_i]/(RT ln 10) + nu_O2 log10 fO2, with melt oxide activities from a solution model (VapoRock Eq. 3). Ideal-mixing oxide activities (older MAGMA) differ by factors 3 to 10 and up to 300 for Si and K, so a generator that wants correct Na/K/SiO ratios needs tabulated outputs of a MELTS-based run, not ideal activities. Both published codes are copyleft (VapoRock AGPL-3.0, LavAtmos GPL-3.0): do not vendor the code; re-implement Eq. 3 with JANAF data, or run the codes offline and store the output tables (licensing of outputs is a decision for Boss).

fO2 during congruent vaporisation of BSE ([R3943], used by VapoRock): dIW +4.5 at 1500 K falling to +1 at 3000 K (VapoRock Section 4.3, READ). VapoRock Table 3 (1900 K, relative to Na = 1, dIW +2.6 to +3.4): K/Na 0.126 (tholeiite), 0.247 (alkali basalt), 0.113 (komatiite), 1.53 (dunite), 0.435 (Bishop Tuff); Fe/Na 0.0138, 0.0076, 0.0173, 0.0087, 0.0052; Si/Na 0.0044, 0.0014, 0.0052, 0.0003, 0.0126; Mg/Na 1.8e-4 (tholeiite), 7.6e-4 (komatiite). Total pressure over BSE melt: about 1e-7 bar at 1500 K, about 1e-3 bar at 2200 K, about 0.1 bar at 3000 K ([R3943: Fig. 1]; Ito Fig. 1).

Temperature classes ([R3059], komatiite, MAGMA): Type I below 2033 K (Na, O2, O, Fe); Type II 2033 to 2588 K (SiO above Fe); Type III 2588 to 2890 K (Mg above Fe); Type VI 2890 to 3168 K (SiO above O2); Type V above 3168 K (silicate oxide dominant). With MELTS-based codes the SiO-over-Na crossover moves down to 2700 to 2800 K for BSE.

Mean molar mass of the vapour: 25 g/mol near 2000 K to 33 g/mol near 3000 K for BSE (F7 computation); use 34 g/mol for fractionated (Na-free) vapour [R2380]. These feed scale height and escape in the atmosphere lane.
