Scope: body-related claims only. Labels follow _brief.md (READ, 2ND-HAND, COMPUTED, SNIPPET, RECALLED, NOT REACHED). Scripts and extracted tables: parts/G-files/G4_* (listed at the end). Reused from Part F: the transcribed Fray and Schmitt Table 2 (F-files/fray_schmitt_table2_transcribed_and_checked.py, NTRS 20210019502).

Key finding in one paragraph: the cold-end vapour-pressure papers exist, but the attribution is off (Blakley et al. is 2024, Planetary and Space Science 244, 105863, and covers only beta-O2 and NH3; N2, CO and CH4 are [R1708]). A 2026 preprint by Grundy's group applies a further +19 percent correction (ccold = 1.19) to all five species, so the "lower by 1.6" and "lower by 1.5" figures are already out of date. The "0 K to 800 K, 12 GPa" range belongs to [R2918], not to [R2137]. The CO2 hydrate quadruple point matches one paper [R4015]. Two licence claims fail: NIST Standard Reference Data (WebBook, XCOM, ESTAR, PSTAR) is copyrighted by the Secretary of Commerce, not public domain, and the NTRS record for JPL Publication 19-5 says it is copyrighted with US-government-purpose use rights, not public domain.
