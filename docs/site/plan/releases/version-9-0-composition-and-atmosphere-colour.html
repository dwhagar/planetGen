Goal

Boss (5:31 PM EST on 2026-11-05): 9.1 is folded into 9.0, so this version holds three things: the composition and atmosphere colour, the view from a planet (phase 2, with its sky data, research and dust model built in 8.2 as foundations) and the gravity map (finished in phase 3 from its foundations in 8.2, 8.3 and 8.5). The dynamic population and trade moved to 9.2 (Boss, 6:00 PM EST on 2026-11-05). The numbers 9.2 onward stay as they are.

What every body, star and atmosphere is made of, worked out from real science, and what follows from it: the colour of the air and the radiation in the air and on the ground. Boss (2026-10-16, 11:25 PM EDT and 11:58 PM EDT): atmosphere composition and the composition of planets, moons, stars and everything is the joint headline of 9.0 with the atmosphere colour; every body has figures on its composition based on actual science, not made up where it can be avoided, and drawing the density first and filling in a fitting composition is as acceptable as deriving it. The atmosphere colour (sky colour and atmosphere colour are the same thing) is the absolute colour, not adapted to the star, and covers gas giants; it shows in the descriptions and replaces the planet colour on the maps. The atmospheric radiation (band transmission and the greenhouse effect from the mixture) and the surface radiation (the dose from the air's shielding and the ground's own composition) are derived the same way. Everything else is built before: the research, the designs and the data tables (the elements, the gases, the materials, the stars) are in 8.1 phases 1 and 2, the star composition function in 8.1 phase 3, and the generation and storage in 8.4 phases 1 to 4, so 9.0 holds the colour, the radiation and what shows them. A major version needs Boss's word. The master note is composition-model.md; the atmosphere plan is atmosphere-colour.md, based on sky-colour-analysis.md. The view from a planet and the gravity map are 9.1, and the backups and repair chain is 8.9 (Boss, 11:26 PM EDT).
