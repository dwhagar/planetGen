Goal

A galaxy is built from one family of a known galaxy type (spiral, lenticular, elliptical, irregular or dwarf): the seed draws the galaxy's stellar mass, then the family from the mass-dependent type fractions, then every shape number from published scaling relations, so no type, size or count is a preset and no named galaxy is copied (Boss, 4:11 and 4:14 PM EDT: no fixed content). The shape, the density model, the potential, the halo, the satellites and the scatters are reused: three steps of the chain come earlier (GEN.378 in 8.1, DB.66 and GEN.379 in 8.3) so that the families only fill in the shape. Design: docs/design/galaxy/galaxy-types.md. One galaxy per database stays; a second galaxy is the later note.
