Rebuilt on 2026-10-16 as a version map (Boss, 1:07 PM EDT: one version per headline, phases inside a version, blockers one phase earlier). docs/TODO.md is the master file: it holds each item's full text, and its "Plan: versions" section indexes every version. This file gives the version's goal, its phases and each phase's build threads with every item's prerequisites in order. A phase holds independent threads: an item's blockers sit in the same thread (earlier in the list) or in an earlier phase. Where the two disagree, TODO.md wins; when an item ships, it leaves TODO.md and its row here is deleted in the same PR (the progress ledger keeps its version). Research notes and judgment calls are in notes.md; the headlines and the ideas not yet filed are in release-roadmap.md.
