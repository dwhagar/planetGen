# Stand-ins ranked by their share of the fudge (GEN.1316)

Status: generated by `scripts/build_fudge_ranking.py` from the fudge table (`src/planetgen/physics/data/fudge_table.json`), the sensitivity matrix behind it and the stand-in registry; do not edit it by hand. A test fails when it differs. It is the share-of-fudge column that the stand-in ranking report of 11-06 was to gain (plan section 5, step 6).

## What the fudge is

A class edge is a line on a finished quantity (a temperature, a pressure, a gravity, a gas share, the water cover). The quantity is made by a chain of generator steps, and each step uses stand-in values (GEN.725) whose source we doubt. The fudge width of the quantity is how far it could move if those stand-ins were wrong by what the registry says they could be. It is **the model's uncertainty, not the world's variety**: a body that sits inside the width of an edge is one the generator cannot yet tell from one on the other side, and the width shrinks by itself when a stand-in is replaced by a measured value and the table is rebuilt.

## How to read the ranking

- **Share** is the share of the *variance* of a quantity that one stand-in makes (the movement of the quantity when the stand-in goes across its registered range, widened by the tier factor, clipped at half the gap between two class edges, squared). The shares of a quantity add to 100 percent.
- **Width falls by** is `1 - sqrt(1 - share)`: how much the width of the quantity shrinks if that one stand-in were exact. It is the research payoff, and it is largest for the stand-in with the most share of the widest quantity.
- **Score** is the unreliability score of the ranking report of 11-06: tier number minus one plus the tweak points (L0 0, L1 0.5, L2 1, L3 3, L4 4); 0 best, 12 worst. A stand-in with a high score and a high share is the one to research first; one with a high share and a low score has a source already, and the source's range is the thing to narrow.
- The sample is 40 systems of seed 20261108, 40 systems of seed 20262108, 40 systems of seed 20263108 (471 body passes) with the end stages of rocky planets and moons made again.
