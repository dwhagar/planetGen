## 3b. Composition as weighted probability tables (Boss, 1:40 AM EDT)

Boss's requirement: every composition category has a table of possibilities with weighted probabilities; every row lists the planet or object classes it supports (the gate); the generator draws among the rows that pass the gate with weights normalised to 100 percent. Part 09 restates every recipe of parts 05, 06 and 07 (plus escape and UV from 04) in that form. Design points:

- **Row:** id, option, gate, weight (a number, or a function of mass, temperature, pressure, redox, insolation, host star, formation distance a_form over the snow line), weight source with verification label, status, notes. **Status:** SOURCED (numbers from a read source), DERIVED (computed from a stated formula), DESIGN Rn (no sourced weight; the physical bracket is given and the generator draws a seeded constant inside it meanwhile, nothing fixed).
- **Combining:** W_i = gate_i x weight_i; p_i = W_i / sum W. Edge clauses can be softened with a normal CDF of stated width. If the sum is zero the table's named fallback row (bare, airless, absent) wins. Level-2 tables draw only after their parent row (joint probability = parent x conditional). Draw order: host star, then the 16 category tables, with the last four reading earlier draws. One seeded random wrapper (GEN.56) is the only random source.
- **Continuous quantities** (redox, metallicity, C/O) are given both as a parametric draw and as a binned table so either can be coded.
- **Size:** 16 categories (a mantle redox, b volatile inventory and water fraction by snow line, c outgassed regime, d retention regime, e sub-Neptune envelope, f giants, g brown dwarfs, h hot Jupiters, i lava worlds, j frost and evolved atmospheres, k comets and KBOs, l white dwarf and pulsar planets, m trace gases, n clouds, hazes and dust, o ground radioisotopes, p host-star UV regime) and **104 rows: 8 SOURCED, 48 DERIVED, 48 DESIGN**. | Label or verdict | Usable in a table or formula | Limit |
|---|---|---|
