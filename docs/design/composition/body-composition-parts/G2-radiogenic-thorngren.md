# G2: check of Boss's second note, radiogenic ratios by body, stellar Th and U, Thorngren mass-metallicity

Scope: claims G2.1 to G2.4 of brief G. Boss's `[cite: n]` tags point to nothing, so each claim was treated as a pointer. Labels as in the common brief (READ, 2ND-HAND, COMPUTED, SNIPPET, RECALLED, NOT REACHED). Scripts and extracted tables are in `parts/G-files/` with prefix `G2-` (`G2-ratios.py`, `G2-thorngren-table2-fit.py`, `G2-mz-compare.py`, their `.out.txt`, `G2-thorngren-table2-parsed.json`). Part E section 2.8b and 3.3 and part C item 2.6 were reused and relabelled where they agree.

Reading key: ppm is by mass. K/U is a mass ratio (the note's "x10^3" is the same ratio divided by 1000). Solar System numbers below are calibration targets only.

## Figures for the generator

All are PARAMETRISATIONS fitted to published data; the Solar System enters only as calibration. Order of draws is free.

### 3.1 Stellar Th (and U) relative to rock-forming elements

Needs stored [Fe/H] and stellar age (both already stored). Quantity wanted: `x_Th`, the birth log abundance of Th relative to Mg or Si against the Solar System formation value.

1. Solar-metallicity discs, |[Fe/H]| up to about 0.1 dex, ages 0 to 8.6 Gyr: `[Th/Si]_birth = +0.149 - 0.0121 * age_Gyr + N(0, 0.053)` dex (Botelho 2019 Table 4: a = -0.0121 +-0.0018 dex per Gyr, b = +0.149 +-0.010, rms 0.053). Equivalent in iron: `[Th/Fe]_birth = +0.082 - 0.338 * [Fe/H] + N(0, 0.040)` (a = -0.338 +-0.049, b = +0.082 +-0.003); there is no age trend in [Th/Fe]_ZAMS (slope -0.0009 +-0.0015 dex per Gyr; mean +0.086 +-0.008, rms 0.047). The reference zero is the Solar System at formation (a normalisation, not a preset: only differences enter).
2. Wider [Fe/H] (about -0.8 to +0.4): no Th data. Use the Eu proxy: `[Eu/alpha] = (B - 1) * [alpha/H] + c` with B = 0.77 to 0.84 (so -0.16 to -0.23 dex of Eu/alpha per dex of metallicity), tight intrinsic scatter 0.025 dex but add 0.06 to 0.08 dex for measurement and catalogue systematics (SD of the detrended fits: 0.071, 0.081). `[Th/Eu] = 0.014 +-0.045` at solar metallicity; below [Fe/H] about -0.2 [Th/Eu] rises with larger scatter (Mishenina 2022, 2ND-HAND), so treat Th/Mg as at least the Eu value there.
3. A single, wider prior if the generator does not condition on [Fe/H]: log-normal in Th/Mg with sigma 0.10 dex (Unterborn 2022, 72 solar twins and analogues; mean 1.21 of the Solar System ratio) up to 0.16 dex (Eu/Mg, 2000 Hypatia stars) and about 0.23 dex for the Nimmo 10 to 90 percent range (+-0.3 dex). Truncate at +-0.5 dex (Nimmo 1 to 99 percent).
4. U: no direct stellar value exists for discs. Derive `U = Th * r_UTh` with the 238U/232Th production ratio 0.571 (+0.037 -0.031) in atoms (Dauphas 2005, via Wang 2020), about +-0.025 dex; the ratio at the star's birth is lower by the decay between the r-process event and star formation, a model dependence (Frank 2014) that has no measurement; carry an extra N(0, 0.05) dex as a placeholder and label it as such. At the Solar System formation epoch the bulk silicate Earth has Th/U (mass) 1.77 (99 and 56 ppb, McDonough 2025) rising to 3.77 now; use only as a test target.
5. K is not an r-process element. Use the volatile rule: K/Mg of the star is log-normal sigma 0.26 dex (Hypatia, 179 stars, includes systematics), but the planet's K/U spread is much larger (below).
6. The sign statement "sub-solar [Fe/H] gives higher Th" is supported only per Fe, and weakly (0.34 dex per dex over a 0.2 dex range). Per rock-forming element ([Th/Mg], what controls heat per silicate mass) the metallicity trend is -0.16 to -0.23 per dex (Eu) and the age trend in the solar-twin sample runs the other way (younger stars richer). Do not hard-code "low metallicity means more heat".

### 3.2 Radiogenic ratios by body (planet scale)

1. Th/U in the refractory-lithophile fraction: log-normal, median 3.77, sigma 0.02 dex (BSE +-3 percent, CI 3.76 to 3.77) as a placeholder; the CI and BSE agreement shows nothing fractionates them at bulk scale. Rock-sample scatter (crustal values) 0.15 dex (part E table). Do not use the Venus (1.5 to 6) or Mercury (2.4 +-0.9) values as evidence of intrinsic bulk scatter: their errors dominate.
2. K/U: `K/U = 70 800 * d_K` with `d_K` log-uniform between 0.03 and 1 as in part E; the only observed bodies fall at 0.03 to 0.28 (Moon 0.032, Earth 0.18, Mercury surface 0.18, Mars 0.28; Venus landers 0.08 to 0.21 with 50 to 100 percent errors). Nothing observed lies above 0.3, so the upper half of the range is supported only by chondrites. If a data-fitted shape is wanted: log10 d_K with mean -0.9 and sd 0.36 over five bodies (COMPUTED), explicitly a five-point fit.
3. Crust enrichment of Th and U relative to the bulk silicate: factors 9 (Mars), 16 to 19 (Moon surface, KREEP-biased), 72 (Earth continental crust, part E). Needed for surface gamma dose: surface GRS values are not bulk values.
4. Part E 3.3 uses `x_Th` sigma 0.22 dex truncated at +-0.5; the data support 0.05 to 0.10 dex for solar twins and 0.16 to 0.23 dex for catalogue Eu. 0.22 is the Nimmo/Hypatia envelope, conservative; keep it as an upper bound and add the [Fe/H] mean shift of 3.1.

### 3.3 Giant-planet heavy elements

1. Mean law: `log10 M_Z[Earth masses] = log10(57.9) + 0.61 log10(M/MJ) + N(0, 0.26)`, valid 19 to 3200 Earth masses, T_eq below about 1000 K, cap `Z_p <= 1` (Thorngren 2016, confirmed in Thorngren and Fortney 2019). At 20 Earth masses it gives M_Z = 10.7 (Z_p 0.54), at 30 13.7 (0.46), at 318 57.9 (0.18); the data at the low end lie above it (GJ 436 b Z_p 0.85).
2. Better for 20 to 150 Earth masses and for high masses (more planets, newer EOS): Chachan 2025 `M_Z = 14.7 + 0.09 (M - 14.7)` Earth masses, scatter 0.66 x M_Z; comparison (COMPUTED): at 20, 100, 318, 1000 Earth masses T16 gives 10.7, 28.6, 57.9, 116; Mueller and Helled FGK (0.1 to 10 MJ) 14.5, 27.2, 42.6, 66.7; Chachan 15.2, 22.4, 42.0, 103. The three agree within about 1.5 for 100 to 1000 Earth masses; the 0.26 dex scatter is the intrinsic figure, the raw residual of the tabulated means is 0.32 dex. Recommendation: use Chachan for the mean, keep the Thorngren 0.26 dex as a cross-check; both stay a population law, not a per-planet fact.
3. Atmosphere is not Z_p: `Z_atm = f Z_p`, `f` in (0, 1]; Jupiter and Saturn f about 0.2, WASP-39 b near 1 (Thorngren and Fortney 2019). Draw `f` uniform on (0.1, 1) as a placeholder (no population distribution exists), and give the atmosphere lane a ceiling of `Z_p`. Stellar [Fe/H] should not set Z_p: Teske 2019 finds no residual correlation. The M_Z against [Fe/H] dependence is nil (tau 0.09).
4. Photoevaporation: apply only to sub-Neptunes and warm Neptunes (below about 1000 K is not the relevant cut: the effect needs high XUV flux and low mass); published envelope Z growth is 0.02 to 0.04 to 0.23 (Malsky and Rogers) and 50 to 70 times metal-to-H (Louca and Miguel 2025) in individual model planets, with a feedback that limits it (metals cool the wind). Offer it as an optional post-process flag with a documented placeholder, not a law.
