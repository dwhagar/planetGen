# Water delivery: the mass law of row b4 (GEN.510), replaced by the formation-distance law (GEN.802)

Status: GEN.510 built 2026-10-24 (lane "Source and tune atmosphere draws"); replaced 2026-10-25 by GEN.802, which wires one joint law into rows b1 to b4 (section 0). Data now `src/planetgen/physics/data/ngpps_formation_water.json` (the earlier `ngpps_water_fraction.json` is gone), tests `src/tests/test_water_delivery.py`. Decision D1 of `tunable-gaps.md` is followed: the formation model is a switch with the Bern population as the MODEL default and a flagged bracket.

## 0. GEN.802: one law in the formation distance and the mass

The mass logistic below (centre 6.1 Earth masses, slope 6.28 per dex) and the snow-line weights of b1 to b3 were two views of one process, and the gate `x >= 1` of b4 contradicted the population (`volatile-delivery-formation-distance.md`). The rows now draw their class from three nested events fitted jointly to the 10,506 planets of 0.5 to 30 Earth masses of the Bern condensed-water population (starting distance from DACE ng76): not dry (water mass fraction f of at least 0.00025), high or rich (at least 0.0016), rich (at least 0.1), each a logit `a + b ln x + c log10 M + h max(0, ln x - ln 0.6)` with ln x clipped to x in 0.03 to 4 and the mass to 0.5 to 30 Earth masses (coefficients in the data file; the separate straight fits of the research note cross each other and a shared-slope ordered logit is biased in the middle range, so the hinge at 0.6 was added: log likelihood -2557 against -2787 and -2767). The nested probabilities are held in order. b1 is the dry share, b2 the low share, b3 the high share and b4 the water-rich share times the plateau `c_b4` (the water-rich planets the plateau removes are counted as high). The mass moves the water-rich share by 0.13 per dex in the logit, so the old mass law is not applied on top. Not tuned to any outcome (tier 6 for the centre, 7 for the width and class split, tweak L0; registry row AT.b4); the thresholds that map f to Ortenzi's cases remain a design assumption. The recipes are not read by the generator yet: no generated value changes, no epoch.

Fit script: `reports/gen802-files/fit_ngpps_formation_water.py` in the project files (downloads the two inputs; the raw files never enter the repository).

Below is the record of GEN.510, whose mass law and data file are gone.

## 1. What changed (GEN.510, superseded)

Row b4 (a water-rich planet, migrated from beyond the snow line) weighed `c_b4 s^3` of the snow-line ratio alone, `c_b4` log-uniform from 0.05 to 0.4. It had no mass dependence, but the migration that brings the water grows steeply with mass. The Bern population synthesis (NGPPS, NG76 nominal, Zenodo 10527170; the group 2 worker of the tunable-gaps investigation, COMPUTED) gives the water-rich share among rocky and water planets of 0.5 to 30 Earth masses with periods 0.5 to 100 d as a logistic in log10 M: centre 0.788 (6.1 Earth masses), slope 6.28 per dex. Bin fractions: 1 to 2 Earth masses 0.02, 2 to 3 0.09, 3 to 5 0.23, 5 to 8 0.49, 8 to 12 0.75, 12 to 20 0.91.

The probability of b4 is now `q = c_b4 L(M)` with `L` that logistic. Since the weights of b1 to b3 sum to 1, the weight `q/(1 - q)` makes the probability of b4 exactly `q`. The logistic reproduces the six bins within 0.03 (a root mean square of 0.020, the tolerance a test uses at 2 sigma). The plateau `c_b4` is the flagged knob: 1 is the logistic as fitted, and it is bracketed 0 to 1, uniform, because published water-rich shares run from at most 3 percent [R3801] to 59 percent [R0715] and 87 percent by formation model, and the synthesis's own plateau is 0.92. The widest range applies where two sources are in question.

## 2. What it is not

- A MODEL label: the numbers come from a physics simulation with disc priors, not from a measurement and not from a Solar System body. The row is partly verified, with that limit written in it.
- It is not tuned to the density modes of [R2774] (0.94, 0.47, 0.24 with their selection): mass and radius alone cannot break the degeneracy with hydrogen-helium envelopes (about 6 informative JWST spectra). The comparison, made with the generator's own M-dwarf planets and the selection model of GEN.505, is section 3 (GEN.1839); it is a check, not a tuning.
- Rows b1 to b3 (dry, low, high delivery by the snow-line ratio) stay labelled stand-ins: their payloads are Earth delivery scenarios and formation distance is not observable. b5 (the continuous water fraction) and b6 (rogue water, not a separate draw) also stay stand-ins; T9 and T11 have no ensemble beyond Earth's budget.
- The stellar-mass scaling of the minimum ice mass (3 (M*/Msun)^0.48 from two figure reads) is not built.
- The recipes are not read by the generator yet: no generated value changes, no epoch.

## 3. GEN.1839: the generator's M-dwarf planets against the three density modes of [R2774]

**What was run (COMPUTED).** `scripts/m_dwarf_density_modes.py` draws M0V to M4V systems with the real generator (the draw of `scripts/class_census.py`, seeds 50000 up, the five types in rotation, no abundance tier), keeps every planet from 0.3 Earth masses up and under 4 Earth radii, puts its own density on the scale of the paper (the Earth-like planet of the same mass, 32.5 percent iron and 67.5 percent silicate, from `bulk_structure.radius_fit_earth`; an Earth-like planet is 1.00 at any mass), measures it with the `m-dwarf` precision model of GEN.505 and keeps the planets that pass (mass better than 25 percent, radius better than 8 percent). The three groups are split at 0.35 and 0.65. 303 systems were drawn (the run was stopped there; 241 of them held a small planet), 1,380 small planets, 1,013 of them measured. The table of the planets is in the project files, `reports/gen1839-m-dwarf-density-modes-2026-11-24/planets.jsonl`, so the numbers can be read again without a draw. The median host is 0.40 solar masses, the range the paper's sample sits in.

| Group (measured) | Generator: share, density, radius, median mass | [R2774]: density, radius | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| rocky (0.65 up) | 48 percent, 0.94 +/- 0.20, 1.08 +/- 0.19, 1.1 Earth masses | 0.94 +/- 0.13, 1.21 +/- 0.28 | density agrees; our spread is the measurement noise of a heavy follow-up cut; radius 0.5 sigma below (the rocky planets we draw are lighter) |

| puffy (under 0.35) | 35 percent, 0.19 +/- 0.08, 2.74 +/- 0.53, 4.1 Earth masses | 0.24 +/- 0.04, 2.85 +/- 0.63 | density 1.2 sigma low, radius agrees |

**Findings.**

1. **Rocky and puffy modes are reproduced.** Their means sit within about one published error, and the puffy group is envelope-bearing in 96 percent of its planets (median envelope 3.8 percent by mass), the rocky group has none. Nothing here asks for a change.
2. **The water mode is not a separate mode.** The measured density histogram (0.1 bins from 0.0) is 11, 129, 129, 89, 53, 43, 70, 87, 89, 96, 95, 61, ...: a shoulder at 0.4 to 0.6 between two broad peaks, not a third peak, and no gap at 0.65. A sample of 34 planets from it leaves 0.60 to 0.70 empty with chance 0.046 (`gap_chance`), so the paper's "no overlap" is a 2 sigma tension, not a contradiction. The generator's middle group is a mixture of planets of 10 to 50 percent water (median 23 percent, 62 of 190 at 30 percent or more) and of water-poor planets with a thin envelope; the paper's group is defined as half water by assumption, and the Bern population has none above 51 percent (section 2 of `tunable-gaps-sources/group2-delivery-retention.md`).
3. **The mass at which water worlds start is wrong for M dwarfs.** [R2774]: water worlds start at 2 to 3 Earth masses and rocky planets run from 0.3 to about 10. Here 91 of the 166 measured planets of the middle group (55 percent) are under 2 Earth masses, and the water-rich share (10 percent or more water) **falls** with mass: 42 percent at 0.3 to 1, 34 percent at 1 to 2, 31 percent at 2 to 3, 24 percent at 3 to 6, 19 percent at 6 and up. The cause is the law of section 0: at `x = a_form / a_snow` of 1 or more it gives `rich` 84 to 100 percent at every mass from 0.5 Earth masses (`delivered_water.class_shares`), and the mass term is weak (it moves the dry share from 0.45 to 0.65 between 0.5 and 5 Earth masses at 0.3 snow lines and nothing at 1 or more). Presumably, around a 0.4 solar-mass star the snow line is near, so many light planets form beyond it (the formation distances were not recorded in this run). The population synthesis for low-mass stars ([R0602], NGPPS IV) has a minimum ice mass that grows with the star's mass (about 1 Earth mass at 0.1 and 3 at 1 solar mass), which is the missing term: GEN.1840 carries it (3 (M*/Msun)^0.48 gives 1.9 Earth masses at 0.4). Re-run the script after GEN.1840; the target is that the middle group has no planet under about 2 Earth masses.

**The "switch" of decision D1 (settled).** D1 kept the formation model "as a switch with the NGPPS logistic as the MODEL default and the flagged bracket 0 to 0.87". No such switch exists in the code and none is wanted: the Bern law is the only draw (section 0), the plateau `c_b4` is fixed at 1 in `class_shares`, and the flagged bracket lives in the registry rows DW1 to DW3 (`gap-filling-plan.md` section 6.41) with their tier and tweak level. The alternative D1 names (a fixed bracket instead of the model) would be the model removed, which the physics-drives rule rules out. So "switch" means nothing as a runtime option; it stays a registry bracket, and the comparison above is the check that the law, read for M dwarfs, still has to pass (GEN.1840).
