## 3. Radiative transfer items (Tier 2)

### 3.1 [R3439] equation (note, "Infrared optical depth line lists and analytical profiles")

The equation in the note, `T^4(tau) = 3 T_int^4/4 (2/3 + tau) + 3 T_irr^4/4 mu* [2/3 + sum_j gamma_vj E_j(tau, mu*)]`, is NOT the published equation (CONTRADICTED as a statement of the paper). Source READ from the arXiv LaTeX source (export.arxiv.org/e-print/1311.6597, [R3439]):

- Semi-grey case ([R1715] form, paper eq. 30): `T^4 = (3 T_int^4/4)(2/3 + tau) + (3 T_irr^4/4) mu* [2/3 + mu*/gamma_v + (gamma_v/(3 mu*) - mu*/gamma_v) exp(-gamma_v tau/mu*)]`.
- Picket-fence case (eq. 98 for several visible bands): `T^4 = (3 T_int^4/4)(tau + A + B exp(-tau/tau_lim)) + sum_i (3 beta_vi T_irr^4/4) mu* (C_i + D_i exp(-tau/tau_lim) + E_i exp(-gamma_vi* tau))`, with gamma_v* = gamma_v/mu*, thermal opacities kappa_1 (spectral fraction beta) and kappa_2, kappa_R = kappa_1 kappa_2/(beta kappa_2 + (1-beta) kappa_1), kappa_P = beta kappa_1 + (1-beta) kappa_2, gamma_1 = kappa_1/kappa_R, gamma_2 = kappa_2/kappa_R, gamma_P = kappa_P/kappa_R, tau_lim = sqrt(gamma_P/3)/(gamma_1 gamma_2).
- Coefficients (paper eqs. 66 to 81): A = (a0 + a1 b0)/3; B = -(gamma_1 gamma_2)^2 b0/(3 gamma_P); C = -[b0 b1 (1+b2+b3) a1 + a2 + a3]/3; D = (gamma_1 gamma_2)^2 b0 b1 (1+b2+b3)/(3 gamma_P); E = [3 - (gamma_v*/gamma_1)^2][3 - (gamma_v*/gamma_2)^2] / (9 gamma_v* [(gamma_v* tau_lim)^2 - 1]); a0 = 1/gamma_1 + 1/gamma_2; a1 = -(1/(3 tau_lim^2)) [gamma_P/(1-gamma_P) (gamma_1+gamma_2-2)/(gamma_1+gamma_2) + (gamma_1+gamma_2) tau_lim - (A_t1 + A_t2) tau_lim^2]; a2, a3, b0 to b3 and the logarithmic terms A_ti = gamma_i^2 ln(1 + 1/(tau_lim gamma_i)), A_vi = gamma_i^2 ln(1 + gamma_v*/gamma_i) are listed in the paper's LaTeX source (Appendix of the derivation, equations labelled a0 to b3 in `Parmentier2013b.tex`).
- Check made: the coefficients were coded from the LaTeX source and evaluated in the near-grey limit (kappa_1 = kappa_2 within 1e-4). They return A = 0.6667 and B = D = 0, and C and E equal the paper's stated grey limits `C -> 2/3 - 2/gamma_v*^2 + 2/gamma_v* + 2 ln(1+gamma_v*) (1/gamma_v*^3 - 1/(3 gamma_v*))`, `E -> gamma_v*/3 - 1/gamma_v*` at gamma_v* = 0.25, 1, 3, 10 (C = 4.6340, 1.5909, 0.9057, 0.6916; E = -3.9167, -0.6667, 0.6667, 3.2333). That is a COMPUTED check that the transcription is right.
- Mean angle and irradiation: `mu* = 1/sqrt(3)` and `T_irr = (1-A_B)^(1/4) f^(1/4) T_sub` with f = 1/4 (global mean) or 1/2 (dayside); the paper says this isotropic approximation is within 2 percent of the exact semi-grey average (READ).

This upgrades part 03's item 3 of the Boss list ("Parmentier and Guillot coefficient formulas NOT REACHED") to READ for the formulas (the opacity tables of [R3440], that give gamma_P and tau_lim by T_irr and Rosseland opacity are still to extract). Use: giants and hot planets get a composition-dependent T-P profile from eq. 98 once gamma_v, kappa_R and the picket-fence ratios are tied to the mixture (the opacity link is the open point).
