# 10 Verification of Boss's second research note (part A: cold ices, clathrates, brines, redox, lava vapour, visible CH4/NH3)

All 13 claims of the second note (cold ices, clathrates, brines, redox, lava vapour, visible CH4/NH3) are checked below; sections 1 to 4 cover claims 1 to 7 and 10, sections 5 to 9 cover claims 8, 9, 11, 12, 13, section 10 is the verdict ledger with counts, section 11 what changes in the generator items. The arXiv search API was rate limited (HTTP 429), so identifiers came from Crossref and web search and the PDFs were then read. Labels: READ (primary text), 2ND-HAND, DATA, COMPUTED, SNIPPET, RECALLED, NOT REACHED. Verdicts: CONFIRMED / PARTLY / CONTRADICTED / UNSUPPORTED / NOT REACHED. Every claim of the note is treated as unverified data.

### 1.3 Claim 4: F&S "bounded at low T by the experimental vacuum limit about 1e-7 Torr"

CONTRADICTED (misattributed). The 1e-7 torr limit is Grundy's own: "we limit our measurement of sublimation to chamber pressures above ~10^-7 torr where the species being studied dominates the composition of gas in the chamber" (base pressure 1 to 2e-8 torr, 3 to 4e-9 with the cryocooler). F&S is a bibliographic review bounded by where earlier experiments exist: beta/gamma O2 2.7e-7 to 1.01e-3 bar (37.6 to 53.09 K) and NH3 1.27e-3 to 6.08e-2 bar (162.39 to 195.36 K; [R3388]), as quoted by Blakley. Blakley dropped lab points near 1e-12 bar where contaminants dominate. READ.

The equilibrium pressure of a solid is fixed thermodynamically and independent of kinetic coefficients. A coefficient alpha < 1 scales both fluxes (detailed balance), so a measured net flux then implies a larger p_vap (flux/(alpha sqrt(M/2 pi R T))). Phi is calibrated with Sc = 1 assumed, so it already absorbs Sc in the redeposition term. Do not scale p_vap by a sticking coefficient in the generator | READ, COMPUTED (logic) |
