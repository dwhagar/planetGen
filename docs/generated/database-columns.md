## `galaxy_shell`

One row per shell that holds content (v91; replaces `galaxy_layer` and `galaxy_column`): the galaxy as a stack of nested spheres, from the core (shell 0) out to `outer_shell_index`. Each shell holds bands -`high_band_index` through `high_band_index` (both polar caps too when `high_band_index` is the shell's top band), the widest run around the plane that holds a sector center clearing the qualification threshold for some longitude (`galaxySkeleton.build_shell_extents`). Always symmetric about the plane. `cell_count` is the number of sectors that run holds, worked out once when the outline is built. A shell with no row has no content at all.

## `region_stats`

region_stats (v161, DB.107): one row per named region of the Galaxy Map (`galaxy/named_regions.py`): a 9-block, `(shell_index, band_index, wedge_index)` (the level, 9, is implied), that holds at least one generated sector. `sector_count` is how many of its sectors are generated and `capacity` how many its outline holds (the share charted is `sector_count / capacity`), `clump_count` how many face-connected clumps those sectors make, and `extent_x_pc`, `extent_y_pc`, `extent_z_pc` and `extent_radius_pc` the bounding sphere of the largest clump (never smaller than the region's framing sphere), galaxy frame, parsecs: what the map frames and draws the region with.
