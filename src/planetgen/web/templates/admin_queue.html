What the queue has learned

For each kind of job the memory it claims at the gate: the typed figure in the code, the figure worked out from what that kind has used on this machine (the job profile), and a figure you fixed. {% if tuning.on %}With enough history the learned figure is the claim; with none the typed one is. A kind marked proven has a full window of finished units from this machine, so its learned figure leads outright and the typed figure is only its default and its clamp (a quarter to four times the typed figure).{% else %}Learning is switched off (PLANETGEN_SELF_TUNING), so every claim is the typed figure.{% endif %} Fixing a figure holds it until you release it; forgetting a kind deletes its samples and the next run claims from the typed figure and measures again.
