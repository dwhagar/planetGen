**Headline:** The share of white dwarfs that are double white dwarf merger products now follows the white dwarf's mass, from the 100 pc sample, instead of one flat 10 percent.

### Changed

- GEN.708: a white dwarf of the old population is a merger product at the share its mass has in the newest sample: 25, 36, 78, 49 and 38 percent in the bins from 0.9 to 1.35 solar masses (Jewett et al. 2024, 204 white dwarfs of the 100 pc sample), and 25 percent between 0.7 and 0.9 (the second peak of Kilic et al. 2018, a stand-in). An old population comes to 8 to 12 percent flagged, inside the sample's 14 percent with a factor of 2, and about half of the white dwarfs of 0.9 solar masses and up, as in the sample. A white dwarf that a system's own star made takes the same draw (it had the blue stragglers' 0.5 percent before). The white dwarf's mass is not redrawn: the initial-final relation already gives more heavy white dwarfs than the sample, so a mass bump on top would count them twice. The flat `white-dwarf` entry of the `merger_product_fraction` knob is gone. Registry rows WM1 to WM3 (section 6.330). Generated values change (which white dwarfs are flagged and rejuvenated); no epoch bump.
