**Headline:** A saved course now has a record that keeps both its forms and can say what changed when it is opened again.

### Changed

- A saved course's record keeps the two ends, a name, when it was saved, the direct line (distance, bearing and mark) and the route (its stops, distance and each hop). It is an entry of the new `course` list kind in the saved-list store, kept per browser like bookmarks.
- Opening it can plan the course again from the current galaxy and list what differs: a different direct distance or heading, a route through other systems or of another length, a route that has appeared or gone, or an end that no longer exists.
- The Save course button and the Courses list that use the record are NAV.4 and NAV.18, still to come.
