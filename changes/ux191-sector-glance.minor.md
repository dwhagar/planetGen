**Headline:** Sector pages now open with an "At a glance" table of what is in the sector and what to avoid.

### Changed
- A sector page has an "At a glance" panel under its map: the star systems and stars it holds, how many of each kind of phenomenon, a Hazards line naming any black hole, neutron star or quasar and the nearest, the facilities outside systems, and a superbubble around it. A row that would say none is left out (UX.191).
- The Galaxy page has a short "At a glance" table above the quadrants: charted sectors, star systems, and how many globular clusters and superbubbles the galaxy has (UX.194).
