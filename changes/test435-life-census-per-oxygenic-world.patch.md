**Headline:** The life census now measures the animal and sentient shares on worlds that have oxygenic life, as Boss chose for GEN.907, and the animal share sits inside its band.

### Changed

- **The life census bands are per oxygenic world (TEST.435).** The shares of life that reach animals and that reach a sentient civilization are taken over the oxygenic life worlds of 300 systems around each of a G2V and a K5V star (63 such worlds on 10-29: 11 percent reach animals, 2 percent sentient), because the default galaxy holds almost none and the bands come from figures for oxygenic life. The share that stops before animals is still taken over every life world of the default systems. Test data and the life class design note only; no band number changed, and they are re-taken after epoch 120.
