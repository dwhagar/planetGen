**Headline:** The life census report now counts the life worlds that have oxygenic life, and the notes on two known test faults name what is still open after the biogenic oxygen change.

### Changed

- **The life census counts oxygenic worlds (TEST.435).** The report says how many of the life worlds make free oxygen and how far they got, because the animal band comes from figures for oxygenic life and only a handful of the life worlds of a generated system are of that kind.
- **The habitable-request note names the open faults (TEST.301).** A request for a habitable world still goes unmet after GEN.855 (the class letter is drawn before the conditions), so the known-anomaly note cites GEN.853, GEN.854 and GEN.646 instead of GEN.855.
