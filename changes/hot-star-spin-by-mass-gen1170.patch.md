**Headline:** The spin of A and early B stars now rises with mass as measured, instead of one speed for all of them.

### Changed
- **Every star hotter than the Kraft break of 1.8 solar masses or more was given the same median speed of 180 km/s (GEN.1170).** Zorec and Royer 2012 put the fast rotators' mode at 160 km/s for 1.6 to 2.0 solar masses and 238 km/s for 3.15 to 3.85, so the median now rises to 268 km/s at 3.5 solar masses, then falls back to 180 km/s at 8 solar masses, where most B stars are slow (Huang, Gies and McSwain 2010: 84% under half the critical speed). The fall is a stand-in (registry row SP17). Star rotation periods of A and B stars change; nothing else does apart from the activity that reads them.
