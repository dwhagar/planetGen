### Changed

- **Habitability follow-ups (GEN.148).** A person's radiation tiers now run Blue to 20 mSv/yr, Green to 100 mSv/yr, Yellow to 1 Sv/yr and Red beyond (they were 50 mSv, 100 mSv and 10 Sv), which closes the gap the research found between 100 mSv/yr and 10 Gy/yr; 50 mSv/yr stays the single-year limit. `L_rad` now reads absorbed dose in Gy/yr (3.06 Sv per Gy, from Mars's 0.236 Sv/yr in 0.077 Gy/yr) instead of treating Sv and Gy as equal. PHI_bio is now the Liebig blend of its four likelihoods (the weakest term to the power 0.65 times the geometric mean to the rest) instead of their product, so ocean worlds score nearer the research's table; a planet of a pulsar, neutron star or black hole is still rated lifeless (10 kGy/yr, Deinococcus's D10). A new `harshness` number (0 to 1, 0.5 for an unscored body) is ready for the tech draw. The design note carries the recomputed worked examples and the microbe dose figures. Scores stored before this release change when a planet is regenerated.
