**Headline:** Worlds with oxygenic life now grow the oxygen that life makes, and every world's life reaches each stage on a timeline drawn from its own temperature and air, so animals and civilizations appear again where the air allows them.

### Changed
- **Biogenic oxygen (GEN.855).** A world whose life splits water (Chlorophyll a, Blue-Optimized Porphyrins) and has a sunlit sea (a surface or hycean ocean) now holds the oxygen its life has had time to make: the balance of organic carbon burial, volcanic reductants and rock weathering, after the first oxygen has used up the reductants of the ocean, crust and air (the reduced gases of the air burn away). Earth's numbers reproduce its 22.7 kPa and a delay of 0.7 Gyr after the first photosynthesis; another light, sea, heat or mantle gives another oxygen. The greenhouse, surface temperature, ozone, radiation doses, atmosphere colour and habitability score are made again from the new air. Retinal, anoxygenic and radiotrophic life make no oxygen.
- **A life timeline from physics (GEN.750).** The three fixed paces are gone. Each world draws five hard-step waits (abiogenesis, photosynthesis, complex cells, multicellularity, civilization), quickened by the Boltzmann factor of its surface temperature, with the first light harvesting earlier for simpler chemistries and the wait for complex cells starting only once the air holds oxygen. A forced technological civilization is a timeline conditioned on finishing inside the world's age. The step means are Boss's choice (0.5, 1.5, 2.5, 3 and 60 Gyr): for oxygenic life at the reference temperature about 41 in 100 worlds reach animals and 2 in 100 a civilization.
- Oxygenic chemistries no longer need oxygen already in the air; they need a sunlit sea. Migration 0156 no longer reads the removed schedule function; it uses the table directly.
