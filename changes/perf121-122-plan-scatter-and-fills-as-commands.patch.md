**Headline:** `planetgen plan` is only the plan; each scatter, fill and clean-up is a command of its own.

### Changed
- **`planetgen plan` draws the skeleton and nothing else (PERF.121).** The scatters moved to a new `planetgen scatter` that takes exactly one of `--mass`, `--luminosity`, `--phenomena`, `--nebula-field` or `--band --down-to N`, with only that kind's options (`--phenomenon-min-mass` for the mass pass, `--bright-star-min-luminosity` for both star passes, `--compact-min-mass` for the phenomena). A scatter draws only where none of its kind is stored and names the clear to run first; the new `planetgen clear-scatter --mass|--luminosity|--phenomena|--nebula-field` empties one kind (the massive stars are refused while the bright stars, split from them at the mass limit, are stored), so a redo is a clear then a scatter. The luminosity pass needs the mass pass stored first and says so. A new plan also empties the phenomena and the nebula field drawn on the old outline.
- **Fills and clean-ups are commands of their own (PERF.122).** `planetgen fill-map-luminosity`, `planetgen rescore-habitability` and `planetgen clean-nebula-field [--apply]` replace `plan --fill-map-luminosity`, `plan --fill-habitability` and `plan --redo-nebula-field`.
- **The Generate page lists a step for each scatter.** The plan job queues `scatter --mass`, `--luminosity` and `--phenomena` as separate steps in the order they have to run, each with its own stage and progress. The Redo scatters form queues the clears (bright stars, then massive stars, then phenomena) and then the scatters; redoing the massive stars redoes the bright stars with them.
- **Removed:** the plan options `--no-bright-stars`, `--bright-stars-only`, `--phenomena-only`, `--bright-stars-down-to`, `--redo-scatters`, `--redo-nebula-field`, `--fill-map-luminosity`, `--fill-habitability` and `--force`, and `tuning.REDO_SCATTERS`. `README.md`, `INSTALL.md`, `docs/cli.md`, `docs/html-interface.md` and `docs/database-schema.md` name the new commands.
