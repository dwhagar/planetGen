**Headline:** The big bodies of a Kuiper-type outer disc now belong to the groups the outer giant sorted the disc into (cold, hot, resonant, scattered, crossing, detached), and each sits on the orbit its group gives it.

### Changed
- **Members carry their scatter group (GEN.1092).** The outer disc of a system the outermost giant acted on has, from the scatter step, shares of its mass in cold classical, hot classical, resonant, scattered-disc, centaur and detached groups (the rest left the disc: thrown out, parked at the giant's Lagrange points, put in the inner belt or caught as irregular moons). Each stored member of that disc now draws its group by those shares and the group sets its orbit: a cold member is stirred a quarter as much as the belt's width would give, a hot one by the whole width, a resonant one sits at the giant's 3:2 or 2:1 resonance (a disc that does not reach them keeps the body cold), a scattered one has its perihelion between the giant and the disc's inner edge, a crossing one has its perihelion inside the giant's orbit, a detached one has it lifted just beyond the disc's inner edge. The group is stored with the member (`belt_members.scatter_tag`, no schema change beyond GEN.1070's table).
- Belts that are not outer discs (the inner belts) have untagged members and the orbits of GEN.1070. The numbers of the group orbits are stand-ins (registry 6.168). The shares the giant takes out of the disc (inner-belt implants, irregular moons, ejected) are not yet handed on to the inner belt, the moons or the rogue builders.
