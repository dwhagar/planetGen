**Headline:** The Galaxy Map's data now lists the groups of nebulae inside each block, ready for the nebula layer.

### Changed
- **Nebula regions in the map's blocks (MAP.173).** A block of the Galaxy Map (9 to 729 sectors) now lists the groups of nebulae inside it: each of its smaller blocks that holds nebulae, with how many, how much space they fill, where their middle and reach are, the share of each nebula family and the commonest class. The largest 600 are listed, biggest first, and each group splits into its own smaller groups when you open that block; the single nebulae stay where they were. Nothing on the screen changes yet (the nebula layer that draws them is MAP.176), and nothing is regenerated. The map's saved blocks are rebuilt once on the next update.
