### Added

Worlds with complex life now have a named dominant species, and technological civilizations have an age and an era (Industrial through Elder). Every spacefaring species founds one polity that claims the generated systems around its homeworld, out to a reach that grows with its age (up to 100 ly), so groups of systems form territories in 3D. `generate.py population` builds all of this from the stored galaxy with no regenerate, and it also runs after every `sector` and `galaxy` run (`--no-population` skips it). New read endpoints: `/api/species`, `/api/polities`, `/api/systems/<id>/owner`, `/api/planets/<id>/species` and `/api/territories`. Schema v44 adds the `species`, `polities`, `system_owners` and `population_state` tables; run `update.sh` (or `migrateDb.py`) and then `generate.py population`.
