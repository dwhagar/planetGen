**Headline:** Black hole, neutron star, nebula and other phenomenon pages now open with an "At a glance" table that starts with the keep-out radius.

### Changed
- A phenomenon page has an "At a glance" panel before its About text: the keep-out radius (or that a ship can pass through it), then its class, size, mass, temperature and the hazard facts for its kind (spin and accretion disk for a black hole, magnetic field and spin for a neutron star, and so on) (UX.192).
- Belt, comet, facility, globular cluster and superbubble pages have an "At a glance" panel too: a belt's distance, density, mass and composition; a comet's orbit type, activity, nucleus, perihelion and period; a facility's kind, placement, host, affiliation and the five ratings; a cluster's or superbubble's radius (UX.193).
