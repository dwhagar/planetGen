### Changed
- The layer-walking scatter passes (stars and phenomena) no longer stop after 100 empty layers. After 5 layers in a row that took nothing they draw the next 10 layers as one group (one expected count over the group, objects placed by sector density), double the group while it places nothing, and go back to single layers after a placement. The same seed gives the same objects with any number of workers. The stage record keeps layers walked singly, layers grouped, the group sizes and layers modified (`tuning.SCATTER_EMPTY_LAYERS_BEFORE_GROUP`, `SCATTER_GROUP_LAYERS`).
