**Headline:** A star with a companion inside 47 AU now loses about two thirds of its gas giants, because its disk loses its gas early.

### Changed
- **A close companion shortens the disk's gas life (GEN.1475).** Kraus et al. 2012 and 2016: about two thirds of binaries closer than about 40 to 47 AU lose their disk gas within a Myr, and the planet occurrence inside 47 AU is 0.34 of the wider and single stars. A star whose nearest companion (or, for a close pair, its two stars) is inside 47 AU now has the chance a core finds gas multiplied by 0.34, so about 66 percent of its giants are not made; its rocky planets are unchanged. About 21 percent of solar-type stars are affected, so roughly 14 percent fewer gas giants and 0.8 percent fewer planets in all; lone stars and wide pairs draw exactly as before. The stand-ins are CD1 and CD2 in the registry (section 6.262).
