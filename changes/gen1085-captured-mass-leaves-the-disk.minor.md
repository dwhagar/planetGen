### Changed
- **Captured mass leaves the outer disk (GEN.1085).** After the planets of a star get their irregular moons, the stored bodies and the remainder of every planet's set are taken out of the star's outer disks, shared by the disks' primordial mass and never more than a disk's remainder (the mass its listed bodies do not hold), so the listed bodies stay whole. A Jupiter-size catch is far under a millionth of its disk, so a disk's mass moves in the last digits; the sum is exact. Inner belts are still not reservoirs: only outer disks give up mass, and the design's other half (a body of an inner belt that is caught keeps its belt designation) stays open under this item. The registry has the rule as row CD1 of section 6.193.
