**Headline:** The light elements in a rocky planet's iron core are now drawn over a wider spread, so some cores come out much lighter or heavier than before.

### Changed

- **Core light elements (GEN.993).** The light elements (sulphur, silicon, oxygen, carbon, hydrogen) of a rocky planet's core were a uniform 3 to 12 percent of the metal's mass. They are now log-normal about 8 percent with a spread of 0.25 dex, clipped to 2 to 25 percent, so Earth's core (5 to 10 percent) and Mars's (about 7 to 20 percent, Yoshizaki and McDonough 2020) both fall inside two sigma. Cores of some planets are larger or smaller than before, which moves their radius, density and heat. Registry row BC18.
