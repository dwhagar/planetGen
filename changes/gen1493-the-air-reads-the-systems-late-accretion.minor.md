**Headline:** A rocky planet's late-accreted veneer now comes from the belts and planets of its own system instead of one drawn number, and its air, water and ground are made again with it.

### Changed
- **The late accretion of a rocky planet follows its system (GEN.1493 slice 1).** The air of a rocky planet drew its late-accreted mass from a log-normal law (GEN.739) because the belts did not exist yet. Once the system is whole, `generation/late_accretion.py` takes the mass from the system's belts and planets (`physics/late_supply.py`, slice 0), at least 5 percent of the law's mean for its mass (planetesimals that never formed a belt) and at most the old 3 percent, and makes the planet's air, water and ground again by replaying `calculate_atmospheric_conditions` from the stream state saved at its last call (`planets.remake_air`) in a stream of its own, so no draw of the run's stream moves. In a census of 240 seeds (246 planets of 0.3 to 3 Earth masses) 43 percent take the floor, 22 percent sit at the ceiling and the median planet takes 0.13 of what the law gave; the pass is 18 percent of generation time (`tests/audit/late_accretion_census.py`, `reports/gen1493-late-accretion-census-2026-11-09.md`). Moons, giants and bodies of no star are as before. Reused, not rebuilt: `late_supply.veneer_fraction`, `influx.Reservoir`, the stream-state replay of `refinish_rocky` (GEN.992) and `refresh_at_final_age` (GEN.839). Stand-in rows LA1 and LA2 (section 6.251), both L3 with burn-down lines; the wiring-register lines of `late_supply` and LS1 to LS3 are gone as the module is now read. No migration.
