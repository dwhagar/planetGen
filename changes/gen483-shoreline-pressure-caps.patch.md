**Headline:** The most pressure an air can keep near the cosmic shoreline now comes from the measured pressures of the Solar System's atmosphere-bearing bodies instead of a design.

### Changed

- **Shoreline pressure caps (GEN.483).** Row d6 capped the air of a body in the four depth windows below the cosmic shoreline at 1e-5, 0.01 and 0.1 bar, none beyond, as a design. The caps are now the upper envelope of the surface pressures of Pluto, Triton, Titan, Venus (with Mars and the Earth under it) by depth below the Berta-Thompson 2025 line, read at the deep edge of each window: 1.4e-5, 0.25 and 8 bar, none beyond. Near-line airs can be thicker than before (up to 0.25 bar in the second window and 8 bar in the third). The window edges stay a design. Registry rows SH1 and SH2.
