**Headline:** The Galaxy Map shows the black holes near what you are looking at, not a pile of far ones.

### Fixed
- **Black holes by a near-field rule (MAP.339).** The map used to draw the 240 heaviest black holes anywhere in front of the camera, which at a 10 parsec zoom were almost all far from the view (237 of 240) and lay in a heap. Now a black hole shows if it is within three view distances of the point the camera looks at, or, beyond that, only if it is heavy enough for how far away it is (the lightest black hole needs more mass the further out it is, as the square of the distance, so a supermassive hole still shows from afar and a crowd of light ones does not). The nearest and heaviest are kept first, and no more than one mark per 120 by 120 pixels of the screen is drawn, a heavy hole following the same rule as a light one. The size of that square is a setting for the browser (`map.blackHoleDensity`, default 120; the page that sets it comes with the preferences screen, UX.236).
