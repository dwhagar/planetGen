**Headline:** The companions of a metal-poor or metal-rich star are now worked out at that star's metallicity, so their sizes match the stars that are built and far fewer systems have to be drawn twice.

### Changed
- **The tree of stars is drawn at the primary's [Fe/H] (GEN.1017).** The tree that fixes the stars of a system and their orbits evolved the companions at the Sun's metallicity while the stars were then built at the primary's. A star of another metallicity has a different radius, so the orbit drawn for it could be too close for the star that was built, and the whole set of companions was drawn again. Giving the tree the primary's [Fe/H] removes most of those first-draw redraws (about 60 to 70 percent by the overlap-free design's measurement). Systems whose primary is not at [Fe/H] 0 and has a companion come out differently.
