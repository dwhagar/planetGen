### Changed

- **Stagnant-lid dynamos (GEN.177).** A rocky planet's magnetic dynamo now lasts 0.4 as long unless it has plate tectonics (a mobile lid). Only a planet with a liquid surface ocean can: from half an Earth mass up it has a 0.6 chance (0.3 above five Earth masses, where the literature is split), and every other rocky planet (dry, ice-covered, vapour, Hycean) is stagnant like Venus and Mars. A watery Earth-mass world still keeping its field at 4.5 Gyr falls from about 38 in 100 to about 22; a dry one never does. Fields stored before this release change when a planet is regenerated.
