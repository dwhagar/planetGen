### Changed
- **Giants are settled once the star's age is final (GEN.961, GEN.976).** A giant's radius was read from the star's age as it stood when the giant was drawn, but the star's age rises afterwards when the planets need time (for life, for instance). About 28 percent of the giants sized from the radius tables had a radius over 1 percent too large and the worst 9.3 percent. After the age is final a giant is sized again at that age and at the mean light of its orbit (an eccentric orbit gives more light than the circle at its semi-major axis), and when that moves its radius by more than a tenth of a percent its density, gravity, interior layers, composition and air are made again to the new radius. The giant keeps its mass, heavy elements and orbit. A system whose giants the age did not move is unchanged.
