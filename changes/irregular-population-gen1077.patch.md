### Changed
- **Irregular moon population (GEN.1077).** New `physics/irregular_population.py`: a giant's capture probability from its close encounters and its gas envelope (rocky planets and planets with an empty zone capture none), the size law left after grinding, and the stored bodies drawn from it with the storage rule of every irregular above 1e16 kg and then the heaviest to 90 percent of the mass, at most 100. A Jupiter with the mean history holds about 8 above 5 km and about 1 above 40 km. The belt ledger gained `mass_below` and a stored list that is not scaled to a budget (`realised`), for populations of a handful of bodies; a belt's own ledger is unchanged. The stand-ins are CP1 to CP10 (6.118). Nothing calls it yet.
