**Headline:** A hot dry body now holds the CO2 of its air at the pressure of the Urey reaction (calcite + quartz = wollastonite + CO2) instead of keeping every gram its volcanoes brought up: 88 bar at 740 K like Venus, 3 bar at 600 K, 0.08 bar at 500 K, limited by the calcium of its crust; on the four bodies of 120 systems it reaches, the CO2 falls from 120 to 223 bar to 0.07 to 9 bar.

### Changed

- GEN.1767: new `physics/carbonate_buffer.py`: `equilibrium_bar(T)` from the printed enthalpies, entropies and heat capacities of calcite, wollastonite, quartz and CO2 of Holland and Powell 2011 [R1981: Table 2a] (88 bar at 740 K against the 92.1 bar Schaefer and Fegley find on Venus [R5313], 257 bar at 800 K against about 275 in the experiments of [R5314]), `capacity_bar` (the crust's calcium, `sulfur_balance.CRUST_CALCIUM_WT`, less the sulfate sink's), and `kept_bar`. `surface_air._shares` uses it for a body with a surface whose carbon is not weathered (never had an ocean, or its water is all vapor, or over water's critical temperature) when its surface is at least 500 K: the CO2 of the air is the pressure of the reaction while the calcium lasts and the surplus after it, recorded as the body's `CarbonSink` so the life stage asks it again at the temperature it ends at. A cool dry body keeps its air as it was, and a body hotter than about 800 K holds nothing more or less (the reaction pressure is over its supply). Sources that disagree: [R5315] call the CO2-silicate reactions of Venus kinetically inhibited, [R5313] take them as the buffer; the newer wins (Boss, 8:41 PM EST 2026-11-16). Stand-ins CB1 to CB4 in section 6.310 (data T1 L0; the 500 K floor and the calcium share T6 L3).
- Measured on four systems that hold the bodies of 120 Abundant-style systems (2,491 bodies) that this reaches (seeds 40080, 40115, 40119 and the others of that scan): the CO2 of Eleo I falls from 223 to 9.2 bar (647 K), of Afredaris III from 120 to 0.07 bar (500 K), a steam world (Eogounar II, 223 bar of carbon) settles at 765 K and 140 bar of carbon where it was at 1,265 K, and a 600 K body with more carbon than its calcium can hold (Eogounar VII) keeps the surplus.
