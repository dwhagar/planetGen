### Changed

**Headline:** Water-loss history uses the same escape efficiency as the atmosphere

- The share of the star's XUV that drives hydrogen (and so water) away in the runaway water-loss history (GEN.639) goes from 0.01 to 0.15, the value the atmosphere escape model already uses (Boss, 2026-11-07). More planets near the runaway limit, mostly around red dwarfs, finish with less surface water; the tests of the history and the climate were re-run at the new value.
