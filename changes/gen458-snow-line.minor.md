**Headline:** Every star now has its own snow line, drawn once around the 170 K blackbody position and stored, and each planet and moon records whether it formed beyond it.

### Changed
- **The snow line is per star (GEN.458).** The fixed 2.7 AU constant (`SNOW_LINE_AU_AT_1_LSUN`) is gone. `physics/constants.py` holds the ice condensation temperature (170 K), `formation.snow_line_au` gives the blackbody position for a luminosity (2.68 AU for the Sun), and `formation.draw_snow_line_au` draws a star's own line around it with 0.2 dex of scatter (cut at 3 sigma, a factor of 4 either way) from the star's own stream, so no other draw moves. The line is stored with the star (`stars.snow_line_km`, schema v132), and the disk walk that counts planets and the outer edge of the disk read it. A close pair's line is the square root of the sum of the squares of its two stars' lines. Compact remnants draw one the same way.
- **Born beyond the snow line.** `planets.born_beyond_snow_line` and `moons.born_beyond_snow_line` record whether a planet orbits at or beyond its star's line (a moon takes its planet's). The water share (GEN.428), the small-body zones (GEN.442) and the moons (GEN.466) read these and the stored line.
- A seed generates different systems from here on (the snow line moves and so does the disk walk's planet count); the generator epoch bump for it is carried by the 8.4 chain (GEN.428 to GEN.430 and DB.69), as Boss decided, so this note does not stamp one.
