**Headline:** Scrolling in on the Galaxy Map no longer stalls or drifts away from the stars: it carries on down to a sector.

### Changed
- **The scroll zooms toward what you can see (MAP.292).** Since the blocks stopped being drawn, the wheel zoomed toward the invisible top face of the tall column of blocks under the pointer, kiloparsecs above the disc, so each scroll tick steered the view up and away from the stars until it reached an empty sector high above the galaxy. It now zooms toward the stars under the pointer, else the point in the disc's plane under it, so the view stays over the galaxy on the way down. A window with no block under the pointer or in the middle of the view (the edge of what it holds) no longer stops the scroll: the next window opens round the point being zoomed toward.
