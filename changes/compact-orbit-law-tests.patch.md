**Headline:** The orbit laws of a black hole or neutron star with a star, and of tight compact pairs, are pinned by tests.

### Changed

- The member-tree generator already draws a compact object's pairings from their laws; tests now pin each one: a compact object with a star is log-uniform in period from 0.3 to 1e4 days, circular under 7 days, marked interacted and clear of the star's Roche lobe; a tight compact pair is log-uniform in axis from 0.003 to 100 AU and circular under 0.1 AU; a pair that would merge by gravitational waves inside the system's age is not drawn; the Kozai cycle of a tight compact pair is quenched by relativistic precession.
