**Headline:** Planetary-nebula central stars are now the 0.5 to 0.7 solar-mass, 100 to 10,000 solar-luminosity, up to 200,000 K cores astronomers observe.

### Changed
- **Hot white dwarfs (the central stars of planetary nebulae) were far too heavy and too dim.** They were generated at 1.1 to 1.44 solar masses, at most 100 solar luminosities and at most 60,000 K. They now draw a mass from a normal distribution about 0.59 solar masses held inside 0.5 to 0.7, a luminosity of 100 to 10,000 solar luminosities and a temperature of up to 200,000 K for class O (30,000 K for B0). Cool white dwarfs are unchanged.
- The old `HOT_WHITE_DWARF_MIN_MASS_SOL` constant is gone, replaced by `HOT_WHITE_DWARF_MASS_RANGE_SOL`, `HOT_WHITE_DWARF_MEDIAN_MASS_SOL`, `HOT_WHITE_DWARF_MASS_SIGMA_SOL`, `HOT_WHITE_DWARF_LUMINOSITY_RANGE_SOL` and `HOT_WHITE_DWARF_TEMP_RANGES` in `physics/constants.py`.
