**Headline:** Life now draws the carbon dioxide of its world down: a biosphere buries organic carbon, and a world with no life holds more CO2

### Changed

- GEN.1821 (the Boss's option 2): the carbonate-silicate weathering law is levelled so that the modern Earth balances **with** its biosphere (3 Tmol of carbon a year net over 6.6 of outgassing, `surface_pressure.EARTH_NET_BURIAL_SHARE`). A world with no life now needs 1.83 times the modern weathering to balance its volcanoes, so its CO2 and temperature are higher; a world with life keeps the CO2 its own burial leaves. The oxygenic classes bury `S - W(p)` (the source of the oxygen law less the oxidative weathering of the kerogen: all of `S` before the rise, `R` at the oxygen balance); classes 2 to 4, which make no oxygen, get burial rows in `life_ledger.json` (the carbon their donors' or hydrogen's electrons fix, four electrons to a carbon, at the ceiling for Class 2 and at the growth yield of GEN.1825 for Classes 3 and 4, 2 percent of it buried: `anoxic_burial_share`). The burial falls in a line to nothing as the CO2 falls from 5 Pa to the producers' 0.5 Pa floor, so a biosphere never drains the air under the floor. The sink is solved again with the burial after the life's air is built (`carbon_burial`, `life._build_air`, `biogenic_oxygen.rebalance_co2(body, burial)`). New stand-in section 6.322 (CB1 to CB5) and citation R5332 (Gebauer and others 2017). The sink search (`biogenic_oxygen.rebalance_co2`) now treats a trial air that the air solver's water feedback sends past twice the world's start temperature on less CO2 than it began with as a runaway the sink cannot be asked about (`REBALANCE_HEAT_LIMIT`; an Earth copy at 442 K had raised an OverflowError in the weathering law at 1.6 Pa of CO2).
