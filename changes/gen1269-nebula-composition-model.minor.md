**Headline:** The generator can now work out what any nebula, remnant or cloud is made of, from its class and the metals of the gas where it formed.

### Added

- `physics/nebula_composition.py` (GEN.1269, part a of GEN.1268): `makeup(...)` gives a nebula's hydrogen, helium and metal mass fractions, the mass of each element in gas and in dust, the dust-to-gas and dust-to-metals ratios, the share of the dust in silicate, iron, carbon, PAH and ice, the ionized, atomic and molecular shares of its hydrogen, and the gas-phase abundances of C, N, O, Ne, S, Ar and Fe, for all 23 nebula and remnant classes. `gas_oh(...)` gives the gas metallicity of a place: the Milky Way gas has its own gradient (0.034 dex per kpc, Boss's answer) with 0.14 dex of scatter drawn from the nebula's key, and a satellite's gas falls inside the widest published range of the two Magellanic Clouds. A solar-metallicity Orion-like H II region comes out with gas oxygen 12 + log(O/H) 8.69 and iron mostly on grains; a metal-poor cloud (SMC) has less than half the metals and dust of a solar-circle one; a cold cloud of the same column holds less molecular gas at lower metallicity; a Wolf-Rayet ring is helium and nitrogen enriched.
