### Changed

- The nuclear stellar disc's mass was a fixed 61 times the nuclear cluster's (the Milky Way's ratio), which made the disc 21 percent of a 4e9 solar mass galaxy's stars against about 3 percent for the Milky Way, and left the thin disc only 23 percent. The disc's mass is now drawn from the galaxy's stellar mass: the centre log-uniform between the two fits of Gadotti and de Sa-Freitas 2025 (arXiv:2509.14319, Eqs. 1 and 2), scattered by a placeholder 0.3 dex. A 4e9 galaxy's disc is now about 6 to 8 percent of its stars. Only a barred galaxy has a nuclear disc, and the disc no longer depends on whether the galaxy has a nuclear cluster. `tuning.NUCLEAR_DISC_CLUSTER_MASS_RATIO` is removed. The Milky Way reference structure keeps its 1.5e9 disc.
