**Headline:** A dense carbon dioxide world with sulfur gases now warms, not cools, when it gains more of a minor greenhouse gas, and hot dense airs no longer read thousands of kelvin too warm.

### Fixed
- **The greenhouse table cooled a dense carbon dioxide world for more of a minor infrared gas (GEN.1854).** On a body of the radiation sweep (4.3 MPa, 30 W/m2 absorbed) the table read 311 K for the model's 361 K and cooled the ground for twice the carbonyl sulfide, sulfur dioxide, carbon monoxide or vapor; it now reads 361 K and warms with each, as the model does. The table is refitted with the model's answers for carbon dioxide airs with minor infrared gases and their doubled twins and a penalty that holds each twin at or above its air. The table is also refitted on the model's answers for airs of today's generator, which it had drifted off: the median error on them falls from 2.5 to 1.7 percent (all airs) and from 1.75 to 1.03 percent (clear cool airs), and the validation airs are kept in the repository and read live by a test.
- **The greenhouse table read hot dense airs thousands of kelvin too warm (GEN.1868).** The first refit of this note put dense hot carbon dioxide and steam airs of 110 to 300 kPa at 9,000 to 11,000 K at 1 AU (the greenhouse factor 1.2 million where the model gives about 200), which took the magnetic field from half of the planets of `test_isotope_record`. On 144 hot airs the fit never saw its median error was 19 percent (24 percent of them more than 50 percent off); the table is refitted with the model's answers for hot rocky airs (0.3 to 5 Earth masses, 0.3 to 1.2 AU) and the median error falls to 4.6 percent (the 90th percentile error from 185 to 23 percent). The hydrogen envelope tests keep their bounds: the refit holds the envelope airs at the answers of the table before it and adds the model's answers for the envelope air of `test_a_metal_rich_hydrogen_envelope_reads_what_the_model_gives` at 160 fluxes and compositions. Median error on the validation airs: 1.57 percent (all airs), 1.05 percent (clear cool airs, bound 1.05), 4.0 percent (hydrogen envelopes, bound 5).
- The seeded pins of `test_surface_air.py` are refreshed for the new table (`NO_STEADY_STATE_KEYS`, `CREEP_KEYS`), and the Earth analog baseline is renewed (`scripts/data/earth_analog_baseline.json`).
