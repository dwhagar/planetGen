The greenhouse table no longer cools a dense carbon dioxide world when it gains more of a minor infrared gas (GEN.1854). On a body of the radiation sweep (4.3 MPa, 30 W/m2 absorbed) the table read 311 K for the model's 361 K and cooled the ground for twice the carbonyl sulfide, sulfur dioxide, carbon monoxide or vapor; it now reads 361 K and warms with each, as the model does. The table is refitted with the model's answers for carbon dioxide airs with minor infrared gases and their doubled twins and a penalty that holds each twin at or above its air. The price is a few tenths of a percent more median error on the generator's ordinary airs (all airs 3.05 percent against 2.5), and the accuracy test of the file states the new figures.
