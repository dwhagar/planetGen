### Changed
- **Region names, anchors and framing spheres (GEN.825).** Every 9-block of the map's block grid (about 780 sectors, 36 pc across) can be given a name of the form "Fardoo Beez Region": the first word is shared by all the regions inside the same 81-block, the second is the region's own place in it, and the names come from the name generator under the galaxy's naming key (no names from the Milky Way or the Solar System). Each region also has an anchor (the centre of its cell) and a framing sphere of 12 pc, smaller only in the few cells the grid squeezes next to the poles (none under 7.8 pc). Nothing shows these yet: the map, the database table and the Generate page follow in the next steps of the chain. No generated value changes.
