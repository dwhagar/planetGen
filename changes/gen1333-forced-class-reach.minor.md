**Headline:** A request for a planet class that names a surface gravity (a forced carbon dioxide world, class G) now draws its masses over the masses that give that gravity and draws up to 80 planets at a place, so it is met in 18 of 24 Sun-like systems instead of fewer than half (GEN.1333).

### Changed

- A request for a class (`FORCE_CLASS` or a slot's `planet_class`) whose conditions name both ends of a surface gravity (G, P, M, H, O, R, F) draws its late masses over the rocky masses of that gravity, widened a factor of 2 each way (stand-in RQ1, section 6.210), and not over 0.05 to 1,000 Earth masses; the draws were spent on giants and on bodies too small for the class.
- A class request draws up to 80 planets at a place before it keeps the last (`tuning.CLASS_REQUEST_MAX_ATTEMPTS`; 40 before). The habitable-world request keeps its 40.
- A forced class G in 24 Sun-like (G2V) systems: 15 met with the gravity window, 18 with 80 draws.
- A forced class P (16 to 35.5 kPa of oxygen at 240 to 270 K) is still out of reach of a request: no draw has any oxygen, because only oxygenic life builds it (the oxygen chain, GEN.1339 and GEN.1340). The request is met or reported unmet, never a P it does not hold, and the test names P as exempt from the 18-of-24 reading with that reason.
- Output changes for forced-class requests only (the drawn bodies); no natural system changes. generation-output: unchanged
