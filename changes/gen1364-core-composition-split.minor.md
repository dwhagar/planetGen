**Headline:** A rocky body's metal core now has a make-up (nickel, sulfur, silicon, oxygen, carbon, hydrogen) that follows how oxidised its rock is and how deep its mantle is, and that make-up decides when its inner core freezes and how strongly its dynamo is driven.

### Changed
- **What a metal core is made of (GEN.1364).** The light elements of the metal, drawn as one lump before, are split into sulfur, silicon, oxygen, carbon and hydrogen. Planets, moons and rogue planets with a metal core store `core_ni_fraction`, `core_s_fraction`, `core_si_fraction`, `core_o_fraction`, `core_c_fraction`, `core_h_fraction` and `core_light_fraction` (mass fractions of the core; the iron is the rest). The mean shares follow the redox of the rock the core formed against (`delta_IW = 2 log10(x_FeO / x_Fe)` from the rock's FeO), interpolated between the cores of Mercury, the Earth [R4608] and Mars [R4833]: a reduced body puts silicon and carbon in its core, an oxidised one sulfur, oxygen and hydrogen. The pressure at the base of the mantle tilts the oxygen and silicon, and a Dirichlet draw from a stream keyed on the body scatters the shares so no other draw of the run moves. The make-up reaches the core's thermal history (`core_heat`): the light elements the solid inner core does not take lower the iron liquidus in proportion to the mole fraction they leave in the liquid (checked against the Fe-FeS eutectic), so a sulfur-rich core freezes later, and the compositional energy of the freezing inner core is the Earth's scaled by the light mass the liquid keeps per kilogram frozen, so a silicon-rich core has a weaker compositional dynamo input and a sulfur-rich one a stronger one. The Earth's mix leaves both as they were. The stand-ins are CX1 to CX7 of the stand-in registry (section 6.213); the silicon partition and the incompatibility of oxygen are read in [R5146], the rest of the partition coefficients, the eutectic and the anchors' pressures are recalled and not yet opened (tier T3). Schema v230 adds the columns, with the units in `db/column_units.py`; the object pages show them. A row made before v230 has none until its galaxy is regenerated and its core takes the Earth's mix (generation-output changed: the new columns, and the dynamo history of every core with an inner core, are part of the golden).
