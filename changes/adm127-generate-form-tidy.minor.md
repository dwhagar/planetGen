**Headline:** The new-galaxy Generate form is tighter: the neutron star options and the skip box sit beside the limit sliders, and Edge is one typed value with its unit.

### Changed

- In the new-galaxy form's Bright stars section, the mass limit and brightness limit sliders, the neutron star and black hole options and the skip box are one row. Each slider has its number box under it.
- The skip box is now "Skip all scatters": ticked, the job runs none of the massive star, bright star or phenomena scatters. The stage list names the box as the reason they were skipped.
- Arm pitch angle, stellar mass, central surface density and compactness show their unit once, not twice.
- Edge and Disk scale length are one text box where you type the number with its unit, such as 12L, 2600pc or 20kpc. The text under the box says what each unit is.
