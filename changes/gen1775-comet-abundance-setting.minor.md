**Headline:** A new galaxy now has a comet abundance setting: the default, Science, gives comets only to stars whose own outer disk a giant scattered, and four higher tiers give the other stars a chance of holding comets.

### Changed
- **Comets follow the star's own outer disk, with a scale setting (GEN.1775).** `StarSystem._generate_comets` no longer rolls the flat 30 percent for every star. On the default tier, `science`, a star holds comets exactly where the generator's own laws put them: where it has an outer-disk belt that a giant scattered (`comet_supply.family_weights`), and nowhere else (a census of 398 stars in GEN.1765 found nearly every star without one). A new Census dial, Comet abundance (`--comet-abundance`, lever `comet_abundance`; `science`, `sparse`, `scattered`, `plentiful`, `abundant`), is shaped like the Class M abundance and the Rogue planets: it is a lever of `galaxy.dials` (so the New galaxy page, the plan's `galaxy_shape_pins` and `--comet-abundance` on a single system take it), the galaxy's stored choice reaches each system as `SystemConfig.COMET_TIER` (`GalaxyShape.comet_tier`), and `generation/comet_abundance.py` gives the chance. A tier multiplies the registered stand-in `SYSTEM_COMET_CHANCE` (CMT8) by 0.25, 0.5, 1 and 2 (`tuning.COMET_TIER_MULTIPLIERS`, new row CMT10, tier T9, tweak L4) for the stars the physics gave none; a star the physics gave comets keeps them on every tier and the physics rate is never scaled. `plentiful` is the chance the generator used before. How many comets a star holds and the shape of each are as they were. `+comets` and the prevalence option still force or scale the draw. No migration: the choice is a pin of the plan.
