**Headline:** A seeded sample of a million systems of each of the eight region classes now checks the catalogue's type counts, region gates, the N tail, every interacting pair and group decay against the registry.

### Added
- TEST.164: the catalogue type audit (`tests/audit/catalogue.py`, run by `tests/test_census_catalogue.py`, `python -m tests.audit.catalogue [report.txt]`, and printed after the multiplicity audit's report by `python -m tests.audit.representation`, which takes `--no-catalogue` for the hour it needs). A seeded sample of 1e6 systems of each region class of the representation audit (a star of the region's population mix from `star_population.sample_mass_band_stars`, its member tree from `scatter_companions.star_tree`), 1e6 white dwarfs of each region class and 1e6 young massive stars is classified and tested: test 10, each type of the registry that is a property of a drawn tree (contact binaries, Algols, Be stars, runaways, blue stragglers, massive merger products, white dwarf companions, close double white dwarfs and their mergers, post-common-envelope pairs, cataclysmic variables, AM CVn, symbiotic stars, high-mass X-ray binaries, young groups) within four Poisson sigma of the registry's default or range in its reference class; test 11, no type at an age no population of its row has, and no contact binary of solar-type stars in the young population or Algol in the old; test 12, the systems with 5, 6, 7 and 8 to 12 stars against the registry's columns; test 13, every contact, semi-detached and accreting pair classified again from its stored radii, masses and orbit; test 14, no stored group older than its decay age. The registry types the audit does not count (a Galaxy count, a planet stage, a sample it does not draw) are listed in the report with why, and the two brown dwarf rows, whose generator (GEN.275) is not built, are counted and reported without a check.
- `scatter_companions.star_tree(row, keep_single=True)` returns a single object's one-member tree, with the flags the draw gave it (a lone Be star, a runaway, a white dwarf that is a merger product); the default is the scatter's, `None` for a single object, as before.

### Changed
- The registry rows of the close double white dwarf and the post-common-envelope white dwarf pair list the intermediate population as well as the old one (the generator draws both for a white dwarf of any age after the young population), and the design note's table says so.
