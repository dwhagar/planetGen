**Headline:** The prevalence suite now also draws whole systems per star type and checks planet occurrence against the rates surveys measured.

### Added
- **The system census (TEST.431) and planet occurrence against observation (TEST.395).** `src/tests/audit/prevalence_systems.py` draws 100 seeded systems of each census star type and checks planets, belts and comets per system, moons per planet, the share with no planet, the share that are pairs, the share with a habitable-class or Blue or Green world, and the share where life reaches each milestone, against `src/tests/data/prevalence_systems_expectations.json` (the prevalence before 4c, commit `0b4d0cc`, each range a marked stand-in). `src/tests/audit/prevalence_occurrence.py` reads the same systems and checks giants, hot Jupiters, cold giants, 1 to 20 Earth-mass planets inside 0.5 AU, Earth-mass planets in the ecosphere and the super-Earth to Neptune ratio against observed rates. Known faults carry their item (GEN.855 the lost complex life, GEN.895 a crash in the interior heat, GEN.866 a hang, GEN.828 and GEN.829 the giant and sub-Neptune gaps against observation) and print in the reports instead of failing.
- **A crash or a hang in a draw is a finding.** The class census also counts a draw that raised and one over its time limit, and tags hot-zone planets that lost their Yellow share since 4c (GEN.896).
