**Headline:** A galaxy plan works out the density integral behind its outline report once instead of twice.

### Changed
- The plan's outline report (the share of stars in planned sectors and the count of sectors that expect a system) used to integrate the galaxy's density over the same grid twice. It does it once; the figures are the same. About 0.2 s off a plan of a default galaxy.
- `docs/design/generation-performance.md` says what is in the "report lines" row and what is left in it.
